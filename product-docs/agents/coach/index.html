Coach agent

How Oliv AI's Coach agent scores every call, recommends one monthly goal per rep, tracks progress every week, how to set it up, and how it compares with other sales coaching tools.

Markdown

Oliv AI's Coach agent runs the coaching cadence for every rep on your team. It scores every recorded call, meeting and deal, then recommends the three behaviors most likely to improve each rep's results that month. The manager sets one goal, and Coach tracks the rep's progress every week until the next monthly cycle begins.

What the Coach agent is

Oliv AI's Coach agent is a revenue coaching agent. Its goals are tied to close rates, stage conversion and deal size, not to communication style or compliance. The manager doesn't have to watch a single recording, and the rep doesn't wait for a quarterly review.

Scoring across four dimensions

DimensionWhat it covers
Pipeline coverageWhether the rep is building enough pipeline to hit their number
Deal executionHow well active deals are run: qualification, stakeholder mapping, next-step discipline
Meeting effectivenessDiscovery quality, positioning, objection handling, agenda control and follow-through
Foundational skillsPresence, active listening, questioning, relating, positioning and checking

The monthly review and the weekly report

Once a month, Coach analyzes each rep's full performance data and produces a review report. It surfaces three focus areas, ranked by expected impact, each grounded in evidence from that rep's calls and deals.

The manager picks the goal that fits and sets it. Coach then breaks the goal into weekly sub-goals. At the end of each week the rep gets a progress report: progress on the goal, wins from last week, and one focus for next week.

Why the Coach agent matters

What it's for

Oliv AI's Coach agent is for teams that want every rep to get better each month, not each quarter. It runs a simple loop: find one gap per rep, reinforce it weekly, and track whether it moves.

How it helps

  • Every call counts. Coach scores all recorded activity, so no manager has to sample calls by hand.
  • Goals come with proof. The three monthly recommendations cite the rep's own calls and deals.
  • The goal stays visible. A weekly report keeps it in front of the rep between conversations.
  • 1:1s open on coaching. The brief lands before the meeting, so nobody starts with a pipeline recap.

What happens without it

Most calls are never reviewed at all. Feedback stays generic, and nothing tracks whether it lands. By week two, the rep drifts back to old habits. A struggling rep often surfaces only after missing quota.

Why buyers care

Sales managers, BDR leads and VPs of Sales are the buyers. Sales managers benefit most directly, with better 1:1 prep and no manual call review. Enablement managers care because they can see which skills drive revenue and which training isn't sticking.

The Oliv AI approach to the Coach agent

Picture an enablement manager ramping five new account executives through their first month. They've finished onboarding and are now running real discovery calls every day. She needs to know who is struggling, and with what, before early habits harden.

The common fallback is sitting in on a few calls per hire and scoring them against a rubric by hand. With five people, that might mean a couple of calls each per week. The picture stays thin, and feedback can land after the habit has already formed.

But every one of those calls is already recorded. The patterns that matter, like skipped next steps or a single contact per deal, only show across many calls. What's missing is scoring at full volume and one clear goal per person drawn from it.

Oliv AI handles this with its Coach agent. It scores every recorded call against the team's scorecards and looks for each new hire's real pattern. Their sales manager reviews three ranked recommendations per person and sets one goal. Every week after that, each new hire gets a progress report, and the enablement manager can see who is advancing.

Coach needs about ten calls per rep to find a pattern. Most teams see their first monthly review within two weeks of connecting their call stack. How that cadence measures up against other coaching tools is covered in How the Coach agent compares, below.

Set up Coach

  1. Connect your call stack. Zoom, your existing call recorder or Oliv AI's own call recording. Coach starts scoring as soon as calls start flowing in. It also connects to your CRM and Slack, with more than 70 integrations in all.
  2. Set your scorecards. Start with the pre-built scorecards for enterprise, mid-market and SMB motions, or bring your own criteria. No engineering is required. Scorecard details are covered in Coaching scorecards.
  3. Let the first monthly review land. Coach needs about ten calls per rep to find a real pattern. Most teams see their first monthly review within two weeks.

No rep training is needed.

Review and set the goal

The manager can accept a recommendation as written, edit it, or override it entirely. Nothing reaches the rep until the manager has reviewed it. The monthly review is for the manager only, and reps see their weekly progress reports, scoped to the goal already set.

Watch progress across the team

Managers see a dashboard of every rep's active goal, weekly progress and improvement trajectory. It shows who's advancing, who's stalling and who needs a direct conversation. Sales leaders see coaching performance at team and org level, including skill gaps that repeat across reps. For a leader's view of this, read about coaching at scale for VPs of Sales.

How Coach works with other agents

Deal Driver

Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent handles the pipeline context before each 1:1. Coach has already surfaced the rep's highest-impact goal, so the conversation starts there. Deal Driver removes the status check, and Coach drives the development conversation.

Content Curator

When Coach sets a goal like handling pricing objections, Oliv AI's Content Curator agent supplies clips from the call library as coaching examples. The manager shows what good looks like from the team's own calls.

Oliver

Oliv AI's Oliver agent updates the methodology and process definitions that feed Coach's scoring criteria. When your process changes, the scoring follows.

Olivia

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is the front-facing master agent for reps. Coach's weekly reports reach reps through Slack, email or the Oliv AI platform.

Limits and things to know

  • The BDR version isn't built yet. Current scoring focuses on AE, AM and CSM calls.
  • It needs call volume. About 10 calls per rep give enough data for a meaningful pattern. More calls raise confidence.
  • It isn't real-time. Coach works on recorded calls after the fact. Live, in-call guidance is a separate tool.
  • It isn't a QA or compliance tool. Goals are tied to revenue outcomes, not script adherence, tone or legal review.
  • It doesn't queue calls to watch. Coach surfaces conclusions, not a list of recordings to review.
  • It doesn't replace the 1:1. It gives managers better material for the conversation.

Common questions

How many calls does Coach need before it can find patterns?

Typically 10 or more calls per rep. More calls improve the quality and confidence of the goal recommendations.

Can the manager override Coach's recommended goals?

Yes. Managers review the goals before they're sent to reps and can modify or replace them.

Does the rep see the monthly review?

No. The monthly review is for the manager only. Reps see their weekly progress reports, scoped to the goal the manager has set.

Does Coach work for BDRs?

Not yet. The BDR version isn't built. Current capability focuses on AE, AM and CSM calls.

What if a rep disagrees with the coaching goal?

The goal is grounded in their own call data and deal outcomes, so the evidence is there to discuss. The aim is a data-led conversation, not a manager's opinion.

Can I use my own scoring framework?

Yes. Start from the pre-built scorecards or build your own from a library of more than 100 criteria. No engineering is required.

How does setup work?

Connect your call recording stack and CRM. Most teams have their first monthly review within two weeks, with no rep training.

Does Coach replace Deal Driver?

No. Deal Driver gives managers pipeline visibility across active deals. Coach gives them rep development visibility. They're often bought together.

Where do reps get their weekly report?

In their usual channel: Slack, email or the Oliv AI platform.

Is Coach a communication style coach?

No. A goal like "be more confident" isn't a Coach goal. Goals tie a specific behavior to a measurable deal outcome.
