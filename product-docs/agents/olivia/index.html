Oliv AI's Olivia agent is the agent you talk to rather than configure. You ask in plain language about deals, meeting prep, pipeline or next steps, and she either answers or hands the work to a specialist agent and reports back. She also monitors your conversations in the background and takes care of things before you ask.

What the Olivia agent is

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is one of two super agents in Oliv AI. The other is Oliv AI's Oliver agent. Oliver owns the back end: the process handbook, the configuration and the rules the org runs on. Olivia owns the front end: the relationship, the context and the execution.

On Home, the Ask tab opens with Olivia chosen. Her pill reads Your Chief of AI Staff, and the Agent Feed tab beside it shows what your agents have done.

She is the same agent for the whole revenue org, not a separate instance per person. She adapts to each rep's accounts and deals, while Oliver keeps her inside consistent company process. In the agent picker she describes herself in one line: "I see everything across your team and dispatch the right sub-agent for each task."

Olivia is a conversation, not a marketplace agent

Olivia doesn't appear in the agent marketplace. She has no card, no detail page, no tools tab and no run history. You reach her on the Ask tab or in a side panel, and her background work shows up as rows in the Agent Feed.

Why the Olivia agent matters

What it's for

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is for revenue people who need the full picture of a deal or account at the moment they act. That picture is split across the CRM, call recordings and email, and someone has to stitch it together.

How it helps

  • Prep that's already done. She surfaces deal context about 30 minutes before a meeting, so you don't rebuild it yourself.
  • Follow-up drafted for you. After a call, the follow-up email is drafted and ready within minutes.
  • Risk raised early. She flags deals with no activity, close dates nearing without progress, or qualification gaps.
  • One place to ask. You tell Olivia what you need, and she routes it to the right agent.

What happens without it

Each piece of busywork is small: prep a meeting, follow up a call, update a field, brief a colleague on a handoff. Added up, it slows the whole team. A quiet deal often gets noticed only when a rep escalates it.

Why buyers care

Olivia is usually bought by a VP of Sales or a RevOps lead. AE and BDR team leads feel the daily value first. Sales managers care about the team consistency that comes from every rep working inside the same process.

The Oliv AI approach to the Olivia agent

Picture a customer success manager who looks after a book of mid-market accounts. One customer has a renewal call at 4pm, and the CSM last spoke to them six weeks ago. Since then the account has had a support escalation, two calls with an account executive and a run of emails.

A common fix is to open the CRM record, skim old call notes, search the inbox and message the AE for anything missed. That likely takes half an hour per call. Even then, a hint of expansion buried in one of those calls may never reach the CSM.

None of this information is new. It already sits in the calls, the CRM and the inbox. What the CSM lacks is someone who holds that history and assembles it at the right moment, without being asked twice.

Oliv AI puts that assembly in a chat beside the work. The CSM presses the suggestion for a renewal call at 4pm. Oliv AI's Olivia agent returns the deal at a glance, who is joining, the risks to watch and talking points. She can call on Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent for account health and its Gold Digger agent for expansion signals.

The autonomy dropdown under the message box opens on Ask before acting, so Olivia aligns on her approach before she takes any action. That setting is one of the points covered in How the Olivia agent compares, below.

Ask Olivia a question

  1. Open Home and stay on the Ask tab. On another screen, press Ask in the header to open her in a side panel beside your work.
  2. Check the agent picker at the top reads Olivia. Her surface is blue to violet with a dotted background. Oliver's is amber to orange with a grid.
  3. Choose how freely she acts with the dropdown beneath the message box: Ask before acting or Act without asking.
  4. Ask your question. Type into the box that reads Ask olivia anything - deals, meeting prep, pipeline, next steps..., or use the microphone. The + button attaches a file.
  5. Check her working. A Reasoning Trace row sits above each answer. It stays collapsed until you open it to read the steps she took.
  6. Read the answer. It can be plain text or a structured result, such as a renewal brief with the deal at a glance, attendees, risks and talking points.
  7. Choose what goes out. Drafted work waits for you to pick a send option, edit it or open a review button.

The suggestion cards

In the side panel, Olivia offers three suggestions about your own day and deals:

  • What does my day look like?
  • Get me ready for my [account] renewal call at 4pm today.
  • Where are my deals getting stuck the most?

What a call-prep answer holds

Ask Olivia to get you ready for a call, and she starts with Deal at a glance: four cards for Deal Stage, ARR, Since Economic Buyer Touch and Deal Health. Below them sit Who's Joining?, Three risks to watch, numbered Talking points and a Researcher brief on a new attendee. A suggestion chip at the end offers the next step, such as drafting the pre-meeting email.

Drafts that wait for your choice

When Olivia drafts an email, she writes two versions. Draft 1 carries a Recommended badge and a Why this version? box, and the second draft says when to use it instead. You choose Send Draft 1 now, send it at a set time, Edit before sending or Send Draft 2 instead.

Under After this goes out, I'll, she lists what follows. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent logs the touch on the deal, and she watches for a reply.

A daily brief works the same way. It groups the work under the agent that did it, with a review button for each, such as Review meeting plan and Review draft from Deal Driver.

Pick up an earlier conversation

Press the menu icon beside the picker to open the history panel. It lists your saved chats with Olivia, with a search icon and New Chat at the top. Oliver keeps a separate list. The pencil icon starts a new chat at any time.

Ask about a row in the Agent Feed

Hover a row in the Agent Feed to show two buttons: a comment button and Clear. Press the comment button and Olivia opens in a side panel beside the feed, with that row outlined. The panel has its own new chat, history and close buttons.

Things you can hand her

You can describe the work in your own words. For example:

  • "Draft the follow-up from my call this afternoon." Olivia pulls from the meeting transcript and drafts it.
  • "This deal has been stuck for three weeks." She engages Oliv AI's Re-Activator agent and surfaces a re-engagement strategy.
  • "Can you prep a mutual action plan for this deal?" She engages Oliv AI's MAP Manager agent.
  • "I just met six people at an event, line up outreach for each of them." She engages Oliv AI's Prospector agent.

Set up what Olivia reads

Oliv AI's Olivia agent works from the data you connect, and each source adds context she can use.

  1. Connect a recording or transcript input. Oliv Notetaker, or an equivalent, is the primary source of her live context.
  2. Connect your CRM. It gives her pipeline and deal history. Oliv AI works as a layer on top of the CRM, alongside your other connected tools.
  3. Connect your calendar. It's how she knows which meetings are coming up.
  4. Add email, if you can. Email is optional, but it adds a lot of context between meetings. LinkedIn or Sales Navigator is optional too, for contact enrichment.
  5. Let Oliver set the playbook. RevOps and leadership configure Oliver with your stages, ownership and handoffs, and Olivia operates inside those rules from day one.

How Olivia works with other agents

Oliver

Oliv AI's Oliver agent sets the process and Olivia runs within it. When Oliver updates the company's methodology or approval process, her behavior updates to match. The Process Graph is the living playbook every Oliv agent reads before it acts.

Meeting Assistant and Researcher

Before a meeting, Olivia coordinates with Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent for active opportunities and with Oliv AI's Researcher agent for new contacts.

CRM Manager

After every call, she surfaces proposed CRM updates, such as field values, stage changes and next steps, for the rep to review and approve. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent handles the execution of those writes.

Gold Digger and Portfolio Manager

Olivia alerts the team when Oliv AI's Gold Digger agent detects an expansion signal. For CS managers she works with Gold Digger and Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent.

Deal Driver

She also alerts reps when Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent flags a manager-level concern on one of their deals.

Context underneath

Every agent draws on the same foundation. Context Capture maps raw activity into account, deal, people and process context, so Olivia knows what changed and what needs attention.

Limits and things to know

  • Not in the marketplace. Browsing agents won't find Olivia. Reach her on the Ask tab, through Ask on another screen or from an Agent Feed row.
  • One history per super agent. Saved chats with Olivia and with Oliver sit in separate lists, so switch the picker to find a conversation.
  • English only. Olivia works in English.
  • No warning on Act without asking. Oliver shows a High Risk banner for this setting and Olivia doesn't, so check the dropdown before you leave a conversation.
  • Hand-offs show in the work, not in a log. A brief groups each piece under the agent that did it, and delegated work carries a "Drafted by" line.
  • She doesn't own process. Stages, ownership and handoffs are set through Oliver.

Common questions

Who is Olivia?

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is the agent you talk to rather than configure. She introduces herself as your chief of AI staff and works through Oliv AI's specialist agents.

Is Olivia the same for everyone on the team?

Yes. She's one agent for the whole revenue org. She adapts to each rep's accounts and deals, and Oliver keeps her within company process.

How is Olivia different from Oliver?

Oliver is the back-end super agent that RevOps and leadership configure. Olivia is the front-facing one the revenue team works with. Oliver sets the rules and Olivia runs the plays.

Why use Olivia instead of the individual agents?

Individual agents are specialists, but they don't know your full situation. Olivia does, so she picks the right agent, gives it the context and brings back the result.

Does Olivia work in Slack?

Yes. She surfaces alerts and drives execution through Slack, Teams, or within the Oliv product.

Can I stop her acting on her own?

Yes. The dropdown beneath the message box offers Ask before acting and Act without asking. It opens on Ask before acting in each conversation.

Does she really watch my conversations?

Her introduction says she monitors your conversations in the background and proactively takes care of things for you.

How do I see what she has done?

Her work shows up as rows in the Agent Feed. In a chat, her answers group each piece of work under the agent that produced it.

Are my conversations with her saved?

Yes. The history panel lists your saved chats with Olivia, with search and New Chat. Oliver keeps a separate list of his own.

Can I ask Olivia from other screens?

Yes. Press Ask in the header of a screen such as the Forecast board, and she opens in a side panel beside it.

What does Olivia need connected?

A recording or transcript input such as Oliv Notetaker, a CRM connection and a calendar. Email and LinkedIn or Sales Navigator are optional.

Is Olivia sold separately?

No. She's the front-facing interface through which the Oliv platform is experienced. For plans, see Oliv AI pricing.
