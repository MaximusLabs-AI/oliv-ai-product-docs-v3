# Olivia agent

What Oliv AI's Olivia agent does, how to ask her questions and hand her work, what she needs connected, and how she compares with other revenue assistants.

- Applies to: All revenue teams
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-21
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/olivia/

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is the agent you talk to rather than configure. You ask in plain language about deals, meeting prep, pipeline or next steps, and she either answers or hands the work to a specialist agent and reports back. She also monitors your conversations in the background and takes care of things before you ask.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it does** | Answers questions and gets work done through specialist agents |
| **Who it's for** | AEs, BDRs, SDRs, CSMs and AMs across the revenue org |
| **Where you find her** | A side panel on the right of a screen, or the full chat page |
| **How freely she acts** | **Ask before acting** (the default) or **Act without asking**, set per conversation |
| **Where alerts reach you** | Slack, Teams, or within the Oliv product |
| **What she needs** | A recording or transcript input, a CRM connection and a calendar |

## What the Olivia agent is {#what-it-is}

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is one of two super agents in Oliv AI. The other is [Oliv AI's Oliver agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/oliver). Oliver owns the back end: the process handbook, the configuration and the rules the org runs on. Olivia owns the front end: the relationship, the context and the execution.

She is the same agent for the whole revenue org, not a separate instance per person. She adapts to each rep's accounts and deals, while Oliver keeps her inside consistent company process. In the agent picker she describes herself in one line: "I see everything across your team and dispatch the right sub-agent for each task."

### Olivia is a conversation, not a marketplace agent {#a-conversation}

Olivia doesn't appear in the [agent marketplace](https://www.oliv.ai/agents-marketplace). She has no card, no detail page, no tools tab and no run history. You reach her through a chat surface, and her background work shows up as rows in the **Agent Feed**.

## Why the Olivia agent matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is for revenue people who need the full picture of a deal or account at the moment they act. That picture is split across the CRM, call recordings and email, and someone has to stitch it together.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **Prep that's already done.** She surfaces deal context about 30 minutes before a meeting, so you don't rebuild it yourself.
- **Follow-up drafted for you.** After a call, the follow-up email is drafted and ready within minutes.
- **Risk raised early.** She flags deals with no activity, close dates nearing without progress, or qualification gaps.
- **One place to ask.** You tell Olivia what you need, and she routes it to the right agent.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Each piece of busywork is small: prep a meeting, follow up a call, update a field, brief a colleague on a handoff. Added up, it slows the whole team. A quiet deal often gets noticed only when a rep escalates it.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

Olivia is usually bought by a VP of Sales or a RevOps lead. AE and BDR team leads feel the daily value first. Sales managers care about the team consistency that comes from every rep working inside the same process.

## The Oliv AI approach to the Olivia agent {#approach}

{/* 5P */}

Picture a customer success manager who looks after a book of mid-market accounts. One customer has a renewal call at 4pm, and the CSM last spoke to them six weeks ago. Since then the account has had a support escalation, two calls with an account executive and a run of emails.

A common fix is to open the CRM record, skim old call notes, search the inbox and message the AE for anything missed. That likely takes half an hour per call. Even then, a hint of expansion buried in one of those calls may never reach the CSM.

None of this information is new. It already sits in the calls, the CRM and the inbox. What the CSM lacks is someone who holds that history and assembles it at the right moment, without being asked twice.

Oliv AI puts that assembly in a chat beside the work. The CSM presses the suggestion card for a renewal call at 4pm. Oliv AI's Olivia agent then lists the checks she runs, one line at a time. She can call on Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent for account health and its Gold Digger agent for expansion signals.

The autonomy dropdown under the message box opens on **Ask before acting**, so Olivia aligns on her approach before she takes any action. That setting is one of the points covered in How the Olivia agent compares, below.

## Ask Olivia a question {#ask-olivia}

1. Open a chat with Olivia. Use the side panel on the right of a screen, or the full chat page, which is reached by link and isn't in the sidebar.
2. Check the agent picker at the top reads **Olivia**. Her surface is blue to violet with a dotted background. Oliver's is amber to orange with a grid.
3. Choose how freely she acts with the dropdown beneath the message box: **Ask before acting** or **Act without asking**.
4. Ask your question. Press one of the three suggestion cards, or type into the box that reads *Ask olivia anything - deals, meeting prep, pipeline, next steps...*.
5. Watch her work. Her row shows *Thinking...* beside her picture, with a card listing each step as she takes it.
6. Read the answer. It can be plain text or a structured result, such as a list of deals with the company, the owner and a suggested next move.
7. If the answer proposes changes, press **Review Changes** to approve them. Accepted changes show as **Merged**.

### The suggestion cards {#suggestions}

Olivia's three suggestions are about your own day and deals:

- **What does my day look like?**
- **Get me ready for my [account] renewal call at 4pm today.**
- **Where are my deals getting stuck the most?**

### Ask about a row in the Agent Feed {#from-the-feed}

Hover a row in the **Agent Feed** and press its comment button. A conversation opens about that row, with the row's agent shown as a chip in the message box and the placeholder changed to *Start talking*.

### Things you can hand her {#things-to-ask}

You can describe the work in your own words. For example:

- "Draft the follow-up from my call this afternoon." Olivia pulls from the meeting transcript and drafts it.
- "This deal has been stuck for three weeks." She engages Oliv AI's Re-Activator agent and surfaces a re-engagement strategy.
- "Can you prep a mutual action plan for this deal?" She engages Oliv AI's MAP Manager agent.
- "I just met six people at an event, line up outreach for each of them." She engages [Oliv AI's Prospector agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/prospector).

## Set up what Olivia reads {#set-up}

Oliv AI's Olivia agent works from the data you connect, and each source adds context she can use.

1. **Connect a recording or transcript input.** Oliv Notetaker, or an equivalent, is the primary source of her live context.
2. **Connect your CRM.** It gives her pipeline and deal history. Oliv AI works as a layer on top of the CRM, alongside your [other connected tools](https://www.oliv.ai/integrations).
3. **Connect your calendar.** It's how she knows which meetings are coming up.
4. **Add email, if you can.** Email is optional, but it adds a lot of context between meetings. LinkedIn or Sales Navigator is optional too, for contact enrichment.
5. **Let Oliver set the playbook.** RevOps and leadership configure Oliver with your stages, ownership and handoffs, and Olivia operates inside those rules from day one.

## How Olivia works with other agents {#works-with}

### Oliver {#with-oliver}

Oliv AI's Oliver agent sets the process and Olivia runs within it. When Oliver updates the company's methodology or approval process, her behavior updates to match. The [Process Graph](https://www.oliv.ai/process-graph) is the living playbook every Oliv agent reads before it acts.

### Meeting Assistant and Researcher {#with-meeting-agents}

Before a meeting, Olivia coordinates with [Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/meeting-assistant) for active opportunities and with Oliv AI's Researcher agent for new contacts.

### CRM Manager {#with-crm-manager}

After every call, she surfaces proposed CRM updates, such as field values, stage changes and next steps, for the rep to review and approve. [Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/crm-manager) handles the execution of those writes.

### Gold Digger and Portfolio Manager {#with-cs-agents}

Olivia alerts the team when [Oliv AI's Gold Digger agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/gold-digger) detects an expansion signal. For CS managers she works with Gold Digger and [Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/portfolio-manager).

### Deal Driver {#with-deal-driver}

She also alerts reps when [Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/deal-driver) flags a manager-level concern on one of their deals.

### Context underneath {#with-context}

Every agent draws on the same foundation. [Context Capture](https://www.oliv.ai/context-capture) maps raw activity into account, deal, people and process context, so Olivia knows what changed and what needs attention.

### Sales development {#sales-development}

A BDR back from an event gets every contact enriched, matched and sequenced before the signal cools. High-intent inbound is qualified and routed in under 30 minutes. See how Oliv AI supports [sales development teams](https://www.oliv.ai/sales-development).

An account manager gets the full account history before every call. Olivia surfaces the revival window before an account goes dark, and expansion signals from calls nobody would review by hand.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Not in the marketplace.** Browsing agents won't find Olivia. Reach her through the side panel, the chat page or an **Agent Feed** row.
- **Conversations aren't saved.** A conversation can't be named, reopened or searched, and leaving the screen loses it.
- **English only.** Olivia works in English.
- **No warning on Act without asking.** Oliver shows a **High Risk** banner for this setting and Olivia doesn't, so check the dropdown before you leave a conversation.
- **No dispatch log.** You see which agent did a piece of work through its "Drafted by" line, not through a list of hand-offs.
- **She doesn't own process.** Stages, ownership and handoffs are set through Oliver.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### Who is Olivia? {#faq-who}

Oliv AI's Olivia agent is the agent you talk to rather than configure. She introduces herself as your chief of AI staff and works through Oliv AI's specialist agents.

#### Is Olivia the same for everyone on the team? {#faq-same-for-everyone}

Yes. She's one agent for the whole revenue org. She adapts to each rep's accounts and deals, and Oliver keeps her within company process.

#### How is Olivia different from Oliver? {#faq-vs-oliver}

Oliver is the back-end super agent that RevOps and leadership configure. Olivia is the front-facing one the revenue team works with. Oliver sets the rules and Olivia runs the plays.

#### Why use Olivia instead of the individual agents? {#faq-vs-individual-agents}

Individual agents are specialists, but they don't know your full situation. Olivia does, so she picks the right agent, gives it the context and brings back the result.

#### Does Olivia work in Slack? {#faq-slack}

Yes. She surfaces alerts and drives execution through Slack, Teams, or within the Oliv product.

#### Can I stop her acting on her own? {#faq-autonomy}

Yes. The dropdown beneath the message box offers **Ask before acting** and **Act without asking**. It opens on **Ask before acting** in each conversation.

#### Does she really watch my conversations? {#faq-monitoring}

Her introduction says she monitors your conversations in the background and proactively takes care of things for you.

#### How do I see what she has done? {#faq-see-work}

Her work shows up as rows in the **Agent Feed**. Work she delegated carries a "Drafted by" line naming the agent that produced it.

#### Are my conversations with her saved? {#faq-saved}

No. Leaving the screen loses the conversation.

#### What does Olivia need connected? {#faq-needs}

A recording or transcript input such as Oliv Notetaker, a CRM connection and a calendar. Email and LinkedIn or Sales Navigator are optional.

#### Is Olivia sold separately? {#faq-sold-separately}

No. She's the front-facing interface through which the Oliv platform is experienced. For plans, see [Oliv AI pricing](https://www.oliv.ai/pricing).
