Converse app

What Oliv AI's Converse app captures, from online meetings and phone calls to email and Slack, what you get after each conversation, and how it compares with other conversation intelligence tools.

Status

Applies to

Converse is Oliv AI's conversation capture app. It records and transcribes your team's online meetings, phone calls and in-person conversations, and captures email and Slack the same way. Every conversation lands in one searchable record, with a summary and next steps ready as soon as a call ends.

What it capturesOnline meetings, phone calls, in-person meetings, email and Slack

What the Converse app is

The Converse app is where Oliv AI keeps the record of what your customers said. Its product page puts the core in a few words: "call recording, transcription and AI summaries." It's built to understand context, so it reads sentiment and intent instead of matching keyword lists.

Converse feeds the same context graph the rest of Oliv AI runs on. Your CRM, your forecast and every agent read from the same record. That's why a call captured in Converse can shape a follow-up email, a CRM update or a coaching note without anyone copying it across.

Where Converse lives in the product

  • Activities › Meetings lists the meetings captured in your workspace in a grid, with My meetings and Team meetings tabs. Each row shows the next step and an AI summary.
  • Activities › Calls lists captured phone calls, with their direction, disposition, owner, company and deal.
  • Clips live in the Library tab of the Enable app, rather than on a route of their own.

Converse the app and Converse the plan

Converse is also the name of an Oliv AI plan. Plans are cumulative: Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell. For what each plan includes, see Oliv AI pricing.

Why the Converse app matters

What it's for

Converse is for revenue teams that want every customer conversation on record, not only the video calls. It gives sales, customer success and enablement one place to find what a customer said.

How it helps

  • Nothing to type up. A summary and next steps are ready as soon as the call ends.
  • Every channel in one record. Meetings, phone calls, email and Slack are captured the same way.
  • The exact moment, found. You search for the objection or pricing concern, then clip it into a library.
  • Context agents can use. Oliv AI's agents read the same record, so follow-ups and CRM updates start from the call.

What happens without it

Most of what customers tell a revenue team never gets written down. Notes depend on whoever was on the call. When a deal changes hands or a renewal comes up, the reason a customer bought is hard to recover.

Why buyers care

Sales leaders care when the team grows past the point where they can join calls themselves. RevOps cares when CRM fields drift from what customers said. Enablement cares when it needs real examples for onboarding and coaching.

The Oliv AI approach to the Converse app

Imagine a head of sales with twelve reps across new business and account management. A buyer at a large prospect raises a data concern on a phone call. Two days later they repeat it by email, then again in a shared Slack channel. Before the quarterly review, the head of sales wants to see every place it came up.

The usual answer is a recorder for video calls and nothing else. Phone calls stay in the dialer, emails in each rep's inbox and Slack threads in their channels. Someone pieces the story together by hand, which can take an afternoon and still misses the conversations nobody recorded.

Buyers have not moved to one channel, and they won't. A tool that records only video meetings leaves the rest of the conversation outside the record. Recording more meetings doesn't close that gap, because capture has to follow the customer wherever they talk.

Oliv AI captures video calls, phone lines and email threads the same way, and Slack too. When your company hosts the meeting, Oliv AI records botlessly through the meeting platform. When someone outside hosts it, a visible note taker joins under your company's name. The head of sales can then search for the exact concern across every captured conversation.

The pricing page lists each capture channel by name: botless meeting capture, phone call capture, email capture and Slack capture. Those four channels are the columns covered in How Oliv AI compares for conversation intelligence, below.

What Converse captures

  1. Online meetings. Oliv AI joins Zoom, Meet and Teams automatically. If your company organized the meeting, recording happens botlessly inside the meeting app. If someone outside organized it, the note taker joins as a visible participant.
  2. Phone calls. Phone call capture puts calls in the Calls list next to your meetings.
  3. In-person meetings. You capture in-person meetings with the mobile app. The Sell and Grow plans also support Plaud, a wearable recorder that travels with your reps.
  4. Email. Email capture adds email threads to the same record as your calls.
  5. Slack. Slack capture brings Slack conversations into that record too.

Capture is always intentional. With in-person capture, the rep decides when and what to record, and capture is participant-aware rather than covert.

What you get after each conversation

  • A transcript. Oliv AI transcribes the conversation and labels every speaker.
  • An AI summary and next steps. Both are ready as soon as the call ends, with key takeaways that link back to the recording.
  • A meeting record in seven tabs. Meeting details splits each meeting into Overview, Action Items, Meeting insights, Recording, Coaching, Attendees and CRM Updates.
  • A call record in four tabs. A phone call opens with Overview, Action Items, Recording and Contact.
  • A follow-up draft. It's ready within minutes, written from the actual conversation.
  • Meeting notes in your CRM. The AI summary and full transcript are attached to the opportunity record.
  • Messages in Slack. Once Slack is connected, Oliv AI sends a meeting summary as soon as each meeting ends, and a copy goes to your email by default.

For the craft side of this, see how to take meeting notes during sales calls.

Find and reuse what was said

Converse is built so key moments are easy to find. Search for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern, then press Clip on the recording to save it to a clip library for coaching, enablement or product.

  • Search for a meeting. Open Activities › Meetings and use search. You can search by the date the meeting was held.
  • Jump in from Slack. A Slack message about a meeting carries an Open Meeting button that takes you to it.
  • Share part of a transcript. Find the minute marker, tick the box for the segment and copy it. Open it in a new tab to start playback from that minute.

Oliv AI's Content Curator agent can also build clip libraries for you. You describe the moments you care about in plain language, and it clips matching moments when each meeting is completed. More on the Content Curator agent.

Get Converse running

  1. Connect your calendar and video conferencing. Oliv AI relies on your calendar for the meetings it captures. See the integrations Oliv AI works with.
  2. Turn on botless recording. If it isn't set up for your organization, contact Oliv Support and connect your video conferencing admin with them.
  3. Connect your CRM. Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho syncs pipeline, fields, owners, close dates and stages.
  4. Connect Slack. The meeting messages start automatically, with no setup of their own. You can turn them off on your Profile Settings page.

Teams that are moving recordings across from another tool can read the guide to migrating from Gong.

How Converse works with Oliv AI's agents

Meeting Assistant

Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent runs on Oliv Notetaker recordings and transcripts. After a call it drafts the follow-up email and proposes CRM field updates from what was said. The prepare for a meeting guide covers its brief before the call.

Content Curator and Coach

Oliv AI's Content Curator agent needs recordings flowing in from Oliv Notetaker before it can clip moments. Oliv AI's Coach agent works on recordings after the fact to help reps improve.

Olivia and Deal Driver

Ask Oliv AI's Olivia agent to draft the follow-up from a call, and she pulls from the meeting transcript. Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent reads from the same record when it flags deal risk.

CRM Manager

Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent attaches the AI summary and the full call transcript to the opportunity record. It keeps deal fields current from call history, as a layer on top of your CRM.

Who uses Converse, and how

Sales leaders

A sales leader gets every customer conversation on record across the team, including phone calls and Slack. Search turns a question like "who heard this objection" into a list of moments. See Oliv AI for sales managers.

Account executives

An AE gets a summary, next steps and a follow-up draft after every call. Before the next meeting, the last conversation is a search away. See how AEs run deals with Oliv AI.

Customer success and account management

A CSM taking over an account can read what sales promised, in the customer's own words. Phone support calls and in-person visits land in the same account record. More on Oliv AI for customer success teams.

Enablement

An enablement lead clips real objection handles and talk tracks into clip libraries for onboarding and coaching. More on sales enablement software.

Why Oliv AI is the stronger choice for conversation intelligence

  • Slack sits in the same record as calls. Converse captures Slack alongside meetings, phone calls and email. Gong documents capturing emails, calendar meetings, or both for each team member. Compare Oliv AI vs Gong.
  • The recording method is chosen for you. Oliv AI records botlessly when your company hosts and joins visibly when someone else does. Avoma's setup asks you to choose Cloud Recording or the Avoma Assistant bot. Compare Oliv AI vs Avoma.

Limits and things to know

  • Some channels are add-ons. Bot-based meeting capture and Slack capture are priced separately. See Oliv AI pricing.
  • Plaud needs a higher plan and its own hardware. Plaud recordings are supported on Sell and Grow, and the hardware is purchased separately.
  • Botless recording needs setup. If it isn't enabled for your organization, Oliv Support works with your video conferencing admin to turn it on.
  • External meetings may need admitting. If an outside organizer's meeting has a waiting room or lobby, someone may need to admit the note taker.
  • Clips sit in the Enable app. The clips route opens the Library tab of Enable.

Common questions

What is the Converse app?

Converse is Oliv AI's conversation capture app. It records, transcribes and summarizes customer conversations across meetings, phone calls, email and Slack, in one searchable record.

Does Oliv AI send a bot to every meeting?

No. When your company organizes the meeting and botless recording is enabled, Oliv AI records through the meeting platform. A visible note taker joins only when someone outside your company organizes it.

Which meeting platforms does it join?

Oliv AI joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams meetings automatically.

Can it record in-person meetings?

Yes. You capture in-person meetings with the mobile app. Sell and Grow also support Plaud recordings, with the hardware bought separately.

How soon is the summary ready?

The summary and next steps are ready as soon as the call ends. A follow-up draft is ready within minutes.

Where do I find past meetings?

Open Activities › Meetings and search. Phone calls are in Activities › Calls.

Does the call record reach my CRM?

Yes. The AI summary and full call transcript are attached to the opportunity record in your CRM.

Can I turn off the Slack messages?

Yes. Turn off the automated meeting messages on your Profile Settings page.

How does Converse decide what's important in a call?

It reads context, sentiment and intent rather than matching keyword lists.

Is Converse a separate plan?

Converse is both an app and a plan. Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell, so each higher plan carries the same capture.
