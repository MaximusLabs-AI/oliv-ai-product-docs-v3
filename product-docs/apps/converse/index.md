How to set up conversation intelligence in Oliv AI: what it captures across meetings, phone calls, email and Slack, what you get after a conversation, and which Converse page to open next.

You want to know what a customer told your team, and the answer is scattered across Zoom calls, phone calls, email and Slack. Conversation intelligence software gets those conversations on record so you can find what was said. In Oliv AI, the Converse app does that job. If nothing is being captured yet, start with recording modes, and otherwise use the table below to open the page that matches your job.

## What the Converse app gives you {#what-it-is}

Go to the Converse app when someone asks what a customer actually said. Its product page sums up the core as "call recording, transcription and AI summaries." It's built to read sentiment and intent instead of matching keyword lists.

You only need to capture a conversation once, because Converse feeds the same context graph the rest of Oliv AI runs on. Your CRM, your forecast and every agent read from the same record, so nobody has to copy a call across by hand.

### Where Converse lives in the product {#where-it-lives}

- **Activities › Meetings** lists the meetings captured in your workspace, with **My meetings** and **Team meetings** tabs. Check the next step and AI summary on a row before you open the meeting.
- **Activities › Calls** lists captured phone calls. Use the direction, disposition, owner, company and deal columns to pick the right one.
- Clips have no route of their own, so look for them in the **Library** tab of the Enable app.

## Why conversation intelligence matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Use conversation intelligence when you need to know what a customer said and you weren't on the call. Get conversations on record across channels first, not only the video calls.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **When a call ends, skip the write-up.** Open the meeting and check the summary and next steps, which are ready as soon as the call ends.
- **When a concern keeps coming up, check one record.** Meetings, phone calls, email and Slack all go into it, so start there before you ask each rep.
- **When you need a real example, search and clip.** Search for the objection or pricing concern, then clip it into a library.
- **When you want a follow-up drafted, check the deal link.** Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent drafts the follow-up and flags CRM updates only on meetings linked to active opportunities.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

A deal changes hands or a renewal comes up, and you need the reason the customer bought. Open the [Meetings list](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meetings/), find the past meeting and read its summary and key takeaways. If nobody captured that conversation, you depend on whoever was on the call, so set up capture before you need it.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

If your team has grown past the calls you can join yourself, start with the **Team meetings** tab. If you run RevOps and CRM fields drift from what customers said, open the guide to [keeping the CRM updated](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/workflows/keep-the-crm-updated/). If you run enablement and need real examples for onboarding and coaching, go to the Clip library.

## The Oliv AI approach to conversation intelligence {#approach}

{/* 5P */}

Imagine a head of sales with twelve reps across new business and account management. A buyer at a large prospect raises a data concern on a phone call. Two days later they repeat it by email, then again in a shared Slack channel. Before the quarterly review, the head of sales wants to see each place it came up.

The usual answer is a recorder for video calls and nothing else. Phone calls stay in the dialer, emails in each rep's inbox and Slack threads in their channels. Someone then pieces the story together by hand, which can take an afternoon and still misses what nobody recorded.

Buyers talk wherever suits them, so a tool that records only video meetings leaves part of the conversation outside the record. Recording more meetings doesn't close that gap. Before you choose a tool, check that capture follows the customer onto each channel your team uses.

Oliv AI captures video calls, phone lines and email threads the same way, and Slack too. When your company hosts and botless recording is enabled, Oliv AI records through the meeting platform. When someone outside hosts, a visible note taker joins under your company's name. The head of sales can then search for the exact concern instead of asking each rep.

You can check the channels yourself on the pricing page, which lists each one by name: botless meeting capture, phone call capture, email capture and Slack capture. Those four channels are the columns covered in How Oliv AI compares for conversation intelligence, below.

## What Oliv AI captures, channel by channel {#capture-channels}

Match this list against where your team actually talks to customers, then set up the channels you need.

1. **Online meetings.** Oliv AI joins [Zoom](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/zoom/), Meet and Teams automatically. If your company organized the meeting and botless recording is enabled, recording happens inside the meeting app. If someone outside organized it, the note taker joins as a visible participant.
2. **Phone calls.** Phone call capture puts calls in the **Calls** list next to your meetings.
3. **In-person meetings.** Capture these with the mobile app. The Sell and Grow plans also support Plaud, a wearable recorder that travels with your reps.
4. **Email.** Email capture adds email threads to the same record as your calls.
5. **Slack.** Slack capture brings Slack conversations into that record too.

Tell your reps that capture is intentional. With in-person capture, the rep decides when and what to record, and capture is participant-aware rather than covert.

## What you get after each conversation {#after-the-call}

Check for these when a conversation ends. The follow-up draft is the one with a condition, so read that line before you promise it to your team.

- **A transcript.** Oliv AI transcribes the conversation and labels every speaker.
- **An AI summary and next steps.** Both are ready as soon as the call ends, with key takeaways that link back to the recording.
- **A meeting record in seven tabs.** [Meeting details](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meeting-details/) splits each meeting into **Overview**, **Action Items**, **Meeting insights**, **Recording**, **Coaching**, **Attendees** and **CRM Updates**.
- **A call record in four tabs.** A phone call opens with **Overview**, **Action Items**, **Recording** and **Contact**.
- **A follow-up draft on meetings linked to active opportunities.** Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent works only on those meetings. On them, the draft is ready within minutes.
- **Meeting notes in your CRM.** The AI summary and full transcript are attached to the opportunity record.
- **Messages in Slack.** Once [Slack is connected](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/slack/), Oliv AI sends a meeting summary as soon as each meeting ends, and a copy goes to your email by default.

## Find and reuse what was said {#search-and-clips}

When you need the moment a customer raised an objection, search for it. The same works for a competitor mention or a pricing concern. Then press **Clip** on the recording to [save it to a clip library](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/clips/) for coaching, enablement or product.

- **Find the meeting.** Open **Activities › Meetings** and use search, for example by the date the meeting was held.
- **Jump in from Slack.** If you have a Slack message about the meeting, press **Open Meeting** to go straight to it.
- **Share part of a transcript.** Find the minute marker, tick the box for the segment and copy it. Open it in a new tab to start playback from that minute.

To have clips built for you, describe the moments you care about to Oliv AI's Content Curator agent in plain language. The agent clips matching moments when each meeting is completed. Before you rely on it, check that Oliv Notetaker recordings are flowing.

## What to connect to get conversation intelligence running {#set-up}

Work through these in order.

1. **Connect your calendar and video conferencing.** Do this first, because Oliv AI relies on your calendar for the meetings it captures. Pick yours from the [integrations Oliv AI works with](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/).
2. **Turn on botless recording.** If it isn't set up for your organization, contact Oliv Support and connect your video conferencing admin with them. [Recording modes](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/recording-modes/) shows what attendees see in each mode.
3. **Connect your CRM.** [Salesforce](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/salesforce/), [HubSpot](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/hubspot/) or Zoho syncs pipeline, fields, owners, close dates and stages. If you skip this, Meeting Assistant has no active opportunity to link a meeting to, so you won't get its follow-up drafts.
4. **Connect Slack.** The meeting messages start automatically, with no setup of their own. To stop them, turn them off on your **Profile Settings** page.

Moving recordings across from another tool? Follow the [migration guide](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/migration-from-gong).

## How Converse works with Oliv AI's agents {#works-with}

### Meeting Assistant {#with-meeting-assistant}

When you want follow-ups drafted after deal meetings, start with [Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/meeting-assistant/). Check that the deal exists in your CRM and the meeting is on your calendar, because the agent works only on meetings linked to active opportunities. Then check that Oliv Notetaker is recording, since the agent runs on its recordings and transcripts.

After the meeting, review the [follow-up draft](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/workflows/record-and-follow-up/) and what the agent flags for the CRM. If there's no draft, check the opportunity link first. For its brief before the call, open the [prepare for a meeting guide](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/workflows/prepare-for-a-meeting/).

### Content Curator and Coach {#with-enablement-agents}

When you want clips built from moments you describe, check that recordings are flowing in from Oliv Notetaker first, because Oliv AI's Content Curator agent can't clip without them. Then describe the moments you care about in plain language, and read more on [the agent's page](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/content-curator).

When a rep needs to improve, get their calls recorded, then open [Oliv AI's Coach agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/coach/). It works on recordings after the fact, so it has nothing to work on until capture is running.

### Olivia and Deal Driver {#with-olivia-and-deal-driver}

When you want a follow-up on request, ask [Oliv AI's Olivia agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/olivia/) to draft it from the call. She pulls from the meeting transcript, so check that the meeting was captured. Meeting Assistant's own draft stays limited to meetings linked to active opportunities.

When you need to check a deal for risk, open [Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/deal-driver/). It reads from the same record when it flags risk, so get that deal's conversations captured first.

### CRM Manager {#with-crm-manager}

When opportunity records fall behind what customers said, connect your CRM and start with [Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/crm-manager/). After a call, open the opportunity record and check that the AI summary and full call transcript are attached. Then follow the guide to [keeping deal fields current](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/workflows/keep-the-crm-updated/) from call history, as a layer on top of your CRM.

## Who uses Converse, and how {#who-uses-it}

### Sales leaders {#sales-leaders}

If you lead sales, start by getting your team's conversations on record, including phone calls and Slack. Then search for the exact objection or pricing concern instead of asking each rep. Read more on [Oliv AI for sales managers](https://www.oliv.ai/sales-managers).

### Account executives {#account-executives}

If you're an AE, expect a summary and next steps after a call. On a meeting linked to an active opportunity, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent adds a follow-up draft. Before the next meeting, search for the last conversation, and learn how AEs [run deals with Oliv AI](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/use-cases/ae-run-deals/).

### Customer success and account management {#customer-success}

If you're taking over an account as a CSM, read what sales promised in the customer's own words. Find the past meetings first, then open each one and read **Transcripts** on the **Recording** tab. In-person visits land in the same account timeline as your calls. More on Oliv AI for [customer success teams](https://www.oliv.ai/customer-success).

### Enablement {#enablement}

If you run enablement, clip real objection handles and talk tracks into clip libraries for onboarding and coaching. More on [sales enablement software](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/sales-enablement-software).

### Why Oliv AI is the stronger choice for conversation intelligence {#compare-stronger}

- **Slack sits in the same record as calls.** Converse captures Slack alongside meetings, phone calls and email. Gong documents [capturing emails, calendar meetings, or both](https://help.gong.io/docs/about-importing-calendar-meetings-and-emails) for each team member. ## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Follow-up drafts depend on the deal.** Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent works only on meetings linked to active opportunities, so plan on no draft from it elsewhere.
- **Some channels are add-ons.** Bot-based meeting capture and Slack capture are priced separately. Check [Oliv AI pricing](https://www.oliv.ai/pricing) before you plan on them.
- **Plaud needs a higher plan and its own hardware.** Plaud recordings are supported on Sell and Grow, and the hardware is purchased separately.
- **Botless recording needs setup.** If it isn't enabled for your organization, Oliv Support works with your video conferencing admin to turn it on.
- **External meetings may need admitting.** If an outside organizer's meeting has a waiting room or lobby, someone may need to admit the note taker.
- **Clips sit in the Enable app.** The clips route opens the **Library** tab of Enable.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### What does conversation intelligence software capture in Oliv AI? {#faq-what}

The Converse app records, transcribes and summarizes customer conversations across meetings, phone calls, email and Slack, in one searchable record.

#### Does conversation intelligence need a bot on every meeting? {#faq-bot}

Not with Oliv AI. When your company organizes the meeting and botless recording is enabled, Oliv AI records through the meeting platform. A visible note taker joins when someone outside your company organizes it.

#### Which meeting platforms does it join? {#faq-platforms}

Oliv AI joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams meetings automatically.

#### Can it record in-person meetings? {#faq-in-person}

Yes. Capture in-person meetings with the mobile app. Sell and Grow also support Plaud recordings, with the hardware bought separately.

#### What do you get after each sales conversation, and how soon? {#faq-summary-timing}

The summary and next steps are ready as soon as the call ends. On a meeting linked to an active opportunity, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent also has a follow-up draft ready within minutes. It doesn't draft for other meetings.

#### How do you search what a customer said across calls? {#faq-find}

Open **Activities › Meetings** and search for the meeting. Phone calls are in **Activities › Calls**.

#### Does the call record reach my CRM? {#faq-crm}

Yes. The AI summary and full call transcript are attached to the opportunity record in your CRM.

#### Can I turn off the Slack messages? {#faq-slack-off}

Yes. Turn off the automated meeting messages on your **Profile Settings** page.

#### How does Converse decide what's important in a call? {#faq-context}

It reads context, sentiment and intent rather than matching keyword lists.

#### Is Converse a separate plan? {#faq-plan}

Converse is both an app and a plan. Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell, so each higher plan carries the same capture.
