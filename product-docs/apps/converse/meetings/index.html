Sales call library: find a past customer meeting by search, filter or a Slack link

How to find a past customer meeting in a sales call library, by date, by phrase, by filter or from a Slack link, and open its recording, transcript and notes in Oliv AI.

Status
Live
Applies to
For converse, Sell and Grow plans
Verified
Verified 3 Nov 2026

18 min read

Your next meeting is close and you need what the customer said on the last one, which is what a sales call library is for. Oliv AI keeps that in the Meetings list under Activities › Meetings, where you search by date or phrase, filter the grid and open the meeting's record. You can also skip the list and press Open Meeting in the Slack message about the call. Phone calls sit in their own list under Activities › Calls, so check there if the conversation wasn't a meeting.

What the Meetings list is

Start here whenever you need a past customer meeting. Open Activities › Meetings, pick the right tab, then read the row. Click the row only if it doesn't answer your question. The meeting's details open in a drawer over the list, with its recording, transcript and notes.

If a meeting you expect is missing, check how it was meant to be recorded. In Converse, Oliv AI's conversation capture app, online meetings are recorded one of two ways: botlessly through the meeting platform when your company hosts, or by a visible note taker when someone else does.

What each row shows

Read the row before you open the meeting, because it may already hold your answer.

  • If several meetings look alike, check Meeting for the title, date and time.
  • If you're deciding what to play, check Duration, which gives the length in minutes.
  • If you only want customer conversations, read Meeting with. It labels each meeting Prospect, Customer, Candidate or Colleague, and you can change a wrong label there.
  • If you need to know what happens next, read Next Step. It shows the step Oliv AI picked out of the call, with a color-coded status icon.
  • If you need the gist, read AI Summary. It shows only the opening, so open the meeting for the rest.

Tabs and views

If you were on the call, start on My meetings, which shows the meetings you were in. If you weren't on the call, switch to Team meetings, which widens the list beyond your own calls. If you keep returning to the same meetings, use Favorites to keep them within reach.

Meetings and calls are separate

If the conversation was a phone call, go to Calls, because phone calls don't appear in the Meetings list. Open Activities and choose Calls from the menu. Use the direction, disposition, owner, company and deal columns to pick out the right call. If you can't remember how you spoke, check both lists before you decide the conversation wasn't captured.

Why the Meetings list matters

What it's for

Use this sales call library when you need to get back to a conversation you've already had. Go to the Meetings list first, before you ask around for who was on the call.

How it helps

  • You have a meeting coming up. Find the last call in My meetings and read its Next Step before you walk in.
  • You remember the day or the words. Search for the date or the phrase, then click the row.
  • A deal just changed hands. Switch to Team meetings and find the calls you weren't on.
  • The meeting just ended. Press Open Meeting in the Slack message instead of looking for the row.

What happens without it

If you skip the list, you rebuild what a buyer said from memory, or you walk in without it. Details like who raised a concern get lost between calls. So check the row before you rely on what you remember.

Why buyers care

Account executives need this before a late-stage meeting, so they open the last call first. Sales managers need it when a deal changes hands, so they start on Team meetings. Customer success needs it at handover, so they look up what sales promised.

The Oliv AI approach to the Meetings list

Take an account executive with a pricing-stage meeting on Thursday. The buyer's finance lead joined a call about four weeks ago and raised concerns about budget timing. The AE wants to hear exactly how that was put before walking back in.

Often the AE scrolls back through the calendar to find the date, then scrubs through an hour of recording. Or they message the sales engineer who was there and wait for a reply. That can easily eat half an hour, and the exact words may still be missed. Under time pressure, the AE may simply go in from memory instead.

The call itself was recorded. What's missing is a fast way back to it at the moment the AE needs it. A recording only helps if it surfaces before the meeting, not after it.

Oliv AI puts that way back in two places. The afternoon before, a Slack message lists tomorrow's meetings, so Thursday's call is on the AE's radar early. In Activities › Meetings, the AE searches for the last call, reads its next step in the row and opens it. From there they can find the exact concern and clip it for the sales engineer.

Oliv AI's own help article shows the date search with an example: a meeting held on June 12th, found by searching for that date. Searching by date is one of the points covered in How Oliv AI compares as a sales call library, below.

How a past sales meeting gets found

You usually remember one of four things about a meeting: the day, the words, the kind of meeting, or the message that told you about it. Start from whichever you remember, because each way in catches some meetings and misses others.

By date, when you remember the day

This is the fastest way in when you know the day. It breaks down when all you remember is "sometime last month". Then you open several meetings before you reach the right one, so check the title and the Next Step in each row first.

By phrase, when you remember the words

A phrase search finds the moment even when you've forgotten the date. Call tools commonly offer it: HubSpot, for one, describes global search for key terms in call recordings in its own product. The weak spot is wording. Search for "budget" and you can miss a buyer who said the money isn't there until next quarter, so try two or three phrasings before you give up.

By filter, when you know the kind of meeting

A filter works by removing the items that don't meet your criteria, which makes a long list short. HubSpot's call index, for example, filters by user, team, call outcome and duration. The risk is that a wrong value hides a meeting completely. If a meeting you know happened is missing, clear your filters and check how it was labeled.

By link, when someone pointed you to it

A link in a message gets you to a recent meeting with no hunting at all. It helps less with an older meeting, because the message has scrolled out of sight. It also can't help if you've turned those messages off.

Which of these Oliv AI gives you

Oliv AI's help article confirms the date search. Its Converse page describes searching for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern. The list's toolbar holds Search, Group, Fields, Sort and Filter, with Quick Filters behind the filter icon. The Slack message carries Open Meeting. If you want patterns across many calls rather than one meeting, that's a different job, and our guide to sales call analytics software covers it.

Find a past meeting

  1. Open Activities from the main navigation and choose Meetings.
  2. Stay on My meetings, or switch to Team meetings to see your team's calls too.
  3. Use Search to locate the meeting. To find a meeting from a particular day, search for its date.
  4. Reshape the grid with Group, Fields, Sort and Filter, or open the Quick Filters panel from the filter icon beside the search.
  5. Click the row to open the meeting's details, with its recording and notes.

Clicking a row opens a drawer with seven tabs: Overview, Action Items, Meeting insights, Recording, Coaching, Attendees and CRM Updates. A round arrow on its left edge collapses it back to the list.

If you're after one moment, search for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern. Then cut the moment into a clip, so you can share it with a colleague before the meeting. For more on getting ready, read how to prepare for a sales meeting.

Classify who a meeting was with

Change the Meeting with dropdown in the row to Prospect, Customer, Candidate or Colleague. The same dropdown sits in the header of the meeting drawer, so you can correct it from either place.

Open a meeting from Slack

You don't have to start in the list. When you get a Slack message about a meeting, press Open Meeting in the message. It takes you to Oliv AI, where you open the green tab the help center points to.

Once Oliv AI is connected to Slack, three messages start arriving with no setup:

  • Summary of tomorrow's meetings. Sent at 4 PM local time, it lists the meetings Oliv AI will summarize the next day.
  • Prep summary. Sent 10 minutes before a meeting, with its title, date, time, participants and account, plus research on the account and contact.
  • Meeting summary. Sent as soon as the meeting ends, with the key takeaways and next steps.

The same messages go to your email by default. To stop them, turn them off on your Profile Settings page. Setup steps are on the Oliv AI integrations page.

Know which meetings get recorded

Which way a meeting is recorded depends on who organized it, when botless recording is enabled for your organization:

  • Your company organized it. Oliv AI records through the meeting platform, such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams. Attendees see the platform's own recording indicator, not an extra participant.
  • Someone outside organized it. A note taker joins as a visible participant, shown with your company's name. If the meeting has a waiting room or lobby, admit it so recording can begin.

If botless recording isn't set up, Oliv Support can help enable it with your video conferencing admin.

How to check your team can find past meetings

Run this check when reps keep asking each other for call links. It uses two numbers: the find rate and the time to find. The find rate is the usability measure known as success rate, the percentage of users who complete a task.

  1. Pick five customer meetings from last month's calendar.
  2. Give each to a rep who wasn't on the call, with only the account and a rough date.
  3. Time how long it takes them to open each meeting's record.
  4. Divide the meetings found by the meetings you picked. Three of five is a 60% find rate.

The weak spots are easy to trip over. NN/g points out that partial success is the hard case to score, and here that means finding the meeting but not the moment. Count those separately. A miss may also mean the meeting was never captured, or that it was a phone call, so check before you blame the search.

Where to read these in the list

You run this check by hand, using what the list already shows. The count badge on My meetings tells you how many meetings you're looking through. The Meeting column gives each row's date and time, so you can match it to the calendar entry.

How to find a past customer meeting by hand

Without a shared list, the job usually runs like this:

  1. Scroll back through your calendar to the likely week, and note the date and attendees.
  2. Message someone who was on the call and ask for the recording link.
  3. Wait for the reply, then open the recording in the tool that holds it.
  4. Scrub through the recording until you reach the moment you need.
  5. Write down what was said, so you don't have to find it twice.
  6. Repeat for every other meeting on the account.

Two things tend to go wrong. The person who has the link may be out, or may have left the company. And when you're pressed for time, you stop at step 2 and go in from memory.

The same job in Oliv AI

Go to Team meetings and search for the date or phrase, then read Next Step and AI Summary in the row. Open the row only if you need more. On the drawer's Overview tab, the key takeaways carry timestamps that link back to the recording, so you can jump to the moment instead of scrubbing. If the conversation isn't there, check Activities › Calls before you assume it was lost.

Worked example: a new account owner catches up on 24 meetings

Say you've just taken over an account and have 2 hours to prepare for your first call. On Team meetings, you find the account's 24 captured meetings. The Meeting with column shows 18 Customer, 4 Prospect and 2 Colleague, and the customer meetings run 40 minutes each.

This is an illustrative calculation with sample figures, not an Oliv AI output.

Remember the column shows only the opening of each summary. If an opening points to a promise or a risk, open that meeting's drawer and read the rest. Go back to the 4 Prospect meetings if you need what was said before the sale. These 24 are meetings only, so check Activities › Calls for phone calls with the same account.

How the Meetings list works with Oliv AI's agents

Meeting Assistant

If you're preparing for a meeting on an active deal, check for the brief before you search the list. For meetings linked to an active opportunity, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends one 30 minutes before the call. It covers the deal summary, qualification status, stakeholders and a recommended agenda. If the meeting isn't linked to an active opportunity, search the list yourself, and use the prepare for a meeting guide to plan the rest.

Olivia

If you only need the follow-up email, ask Oliv AI's Olivia agent to draft it from the call instead of opening the meeting. She pulls from the meeting transcript. If the email quotes the buyer's exact words, open the meeting and check them before you send it.

Deal Driver

If Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent flags a deal at risk, open the meetings behind it from Team meetings. The agent reads the same record your captured meetings feed. Check what was actually said before you raise the deal with the rep.

Content Curator

If you keep hunting for the same kind of moment, such as a pricing objection for coaching, describe it to Oliv AI's Content Curator agent rather than clipping by hand. The agent watches every new recording and clips the moments your team has described. Before you share a clip, follow its link back to the full source recording and check the context.

Limits and things to know

  • Phone calls are in a separate list. Look under Activities › Calls for them.
  • The AI Summary column shows the opening only. Open the meeting's drawer for the full summary.
  • A lobby can stop a recording. For meetings organized outside your company, the note taker may need admitting before recording can begin.
  • Botless recording needs enabling. If it isn't set up for your organization, Oliv Support helps your video conferencing admin turn it on.
  • Clips live in Enable. The clips route opens the Library tab of the Enable app, which is separate from the Meetings list.
  • Slack messages are on by default. They also go to your email. Turn them off in Profile Settings.

Common questions

Where is the Meetings list?

Under Activities › Meetings in the main navigation. The Activities menu also holds Calls.

How do you find a past customer meeting?

Open Activities › Meetings, search for the date or a phrase, and click the row. If you weren't on the call, switch to Team meetings first.

How do I find a meeting from a specific day?

Search the Meetings list for the date. Oliv AI's help article uses a meeting held on June 12th as the example.

Can I search for something that was said?

Yes. Search for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern, then cut the moment into a clip to share it.

Can managers see their team's sales calls?

Yes. Switch from My meetings to Team meetings to see meetings beyond your own calls. Phone calls are in the Calls list.

What does each row show?

The meeting title, date and time, its duration, who it was with, the next step and the start of the AI summary.

Are phone calls in the Meetings list?

No. Captured phone calls have their own grid under Activities › Calls, so look there if you can't find a conversation.

How do I pick out customer meetings from internal ones?

Read the Meeting with column, which labels each meeting Prospect, Customer, Candidate or Colleague. If a label is wrong, change it from the dropdown.

How do I open a meeting from Slack?

Press Open Meeting in the Slack message about that meeting. Oliv AI opens the meeting for you.

When does the list of tomorrow's meetings arrive?

At 4 PM local time the day before, once Oliv AI is connected to Slack.

When does the prep summary arrive?

Ten minutes before the meeting. It shows the meeting details and research on the account and contact.

Can I stop the Slack messages?

Yes. Turn them off on your Profile Settings page.

Do I need to set up these messages?

No. They start automatically once Slack is connected, and they also go to your email by default.

Why did a meeting not record?

If someone outside your company organized it, the note taker may have been waiting in the lobby. It needs admitting before recording can begin.
