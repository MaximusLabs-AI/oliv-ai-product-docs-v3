What the Meetings list is

The Meetings list shows the meetings captured in your workspace in a grid. Each row is the way into one meeting record, with its recording, transcript and notes. Clicking a row opens that meeting's details in a drawer over the list.

The list belongs to Converse, Oliv AI's conversation capture app. Online meetings are recorded one of two ways: botlessly through the meeting platform when your company hosts, or by a visible note taker when someone else does.

What each row shows

  • Meeting. A video icon, the meeting title, and the date and time it was held.
  • Duration. How long the meeting ran, in minutes.
  • Meeting with. A dropdown that classifies the meeting as Prospect, Customer, Candidate or Colleague.
  • Next Step. The next step Oliv AI picked out of the call, with a colour-coded status icon.
  • AI Summary. The opening of the meeting's AI summary.

Tabs and views

My meetings shows the meetings you were in, with a count badge. Team meetings widens the list beyond your own calls, which is how a manager reviews the team's week. Favorites keeps the meetings you return to most within reach.

Meetings and calls are separate

Phone calls don't appear in the Meetings list. Activities opens a menu with two entries, Meetings and Calls. The Calls list works the same way, with its own columns for direction, disposition, owner, company and deal.

Why the Meetings list matters

What it's for

The Meetings list is for getting back to a conversation you've already had. It gives every rep one place to find a customer meeting, without asking who was on the call.

How it helps

  • One place to look. Every captured meeting in the workspace is in the same grid.
  • The outcome at a glance. Each row shows the next step and the start of the AI summary.
  • Found by date or phrase. You search for the day it happened or the words that were said.
  • One press from Slack. The message after a meeting links straight to it.

What happens without it

A recording nobody can find is close to a recording nobody made. Reps rebuild what a buyer said from memory, or walk into the next meeting without it. Small details, like who raised a concern, get lost between calls.

Why buyers care

Account executives feel it before a late-stage meeting, when the last conversation matters most. Sales managers feel it when a deal changes hands. Customer success feels it at handover, when sales' promises need to be on record.

The Oliv AI approach to the Meetings list

Take an account executive with a pricing-stage meeting on Thursday. The buyer's finance lead joined a call about four weeks ago and raised concerns about budget timing. The AE wants to hear exactly how that was put before walking back in.

Often the AE scrolls back through the calendar to find the date, then scrubs through an hour of recording. Or they message the sales engineer who was there and wait for a reply. That can easily eat half an hour, and the exact words may still be missed. Under time pressure, the AE may simply go in from memory instead.

The call itself was recorded. What's missing is a fast way back to it at the moment the AE needs it. A recording only helps if it surfaces before the meeting, not after it.

Oliv AI puts that way back in two places. The afternoon before, a Slack message lists tomorrow's meetings, so Thursday's call is on the AE's radar early. In Activities › Meetings, the AE searches for the last call, reads its next step in the row and opens it. From there they can find the exact concern and clip it for the sales engineer.

Oliv AI's own help article shows the date search with a worked example: a meeting held on June 12th, found by searching for that date. Searching by date is one of the points covered in How Oliv AI compares for finding past sales meetings, below.

Find a past meeting

  1. Open Activities from the main navigation and choose Meetings.
  2. Stay on My meetings, or switch to Team meetings to see your team's calls too.
  3. Use Search to locate the meeting. To find a meeting from a particular day, search for its date.
  4. Reshape the grid with Group, Fields, Sort and Filter, or open the Quick Filters panel from the filter icon beside the search.
  5. Click the row to open the meeting's details, with its recording and notes.

Clicking a row opens a drawer with seven tabs: Overview, Action Items, Meeting insights, Recording, Coaching, Attendees and CRM Updates. A round arrow on its left edge collapses it back to the list.

If you're after one moment, search for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern. Then clip the moment into a clip library, so you can share it with a colleague before the meeting. For more on getting ready, read how to prepare for a sales meeting.

Classify who a meeting was with

Change the Meeting with dropdown in the row to Prospect, Customer, Candidate or Colleague. The same dropdown sits in the header of the meeting drawer, so you can correct it from either place.

Open a meeting from Slack

You don't have to start in the list. When you get a Slack message about a meeting, press Open Meeting in the message. It takes you to Oliv AI, where you open the green tab the help center points to.

Once Oliv AI is connected to Slack, three messages start arriving with no setup:

  • Summary of tomorrow's meetings. Sent at 4 PM local time, it lists the meetings Oliv AI will summarize the next day.
  • Prep summary. Sent 10 minutes before a meeting, with its title, date, time, participants and account, plus research on the account and contact.
  • Meeting summary. Sent as soon as the meeting ends, with the key takeaways and next steps.

The same messages go to your email by default. To stop them, turn them off on your Profile Settings page. Setup steps are on the Oliv AI integrations page.

Know which meetings get recorded

Which way a meeting is recorded depends on who organized it, when botless recording is enabled for your organization:

  • Your company organized it. Oliv AI records through the meeting platform, such as Zoom or Microsoft Teams. Attendees see the platform's own recording indicator, not an extra participant.
  • Someone outside organized it. A note taker joins as a visible participant, shown with your company's name. If the meeting has a waiting room or lobby, admit it so recording can begin.

If botless recording isn't set up, Oliv Support can help enable it with your video conferencing admin.

How the Meetings list works with Oliv AI's agents

Meeting Assistant

For meetings linked to an active opportunity, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends a brief 30 minutes before the call. It covers the deal summary, qualification status, stakeholders and a recommended agenda. The prepare for a meeting guide walks through it.

Olivia

You can ask Oliv AI's Olivia agent to draft the follow-up from a call, and she pulls from the meeting transcript. It saves opening the meeting yourself when you only need the email.

Deal Driver

Every captured meeting feeds the same record that Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent reads to flag deals at risk. A manager reviewing a flagged deal can open the meetings behind it from the Team meetings tab.

Content Curator

Oliv AI's Content Curator agent watches every new recording and clips the moments your team has described. Its clips keep a link back to the full source recording.

Why Oliv AI is the stronger choice for finding past sales meetings

  • Tomorrow's meetings, in Slack, the day before. Oliv AI sends the list at 4 PM local time. Gong's daily digest is an email for team members with calls that day. Compare Oliv AI vs Gong.

Limits and things to know

  • Phone calls are in a separate list. Look under Activities › Calls for them.
  • A lobby can stop a recording. For meetings organized outside your company, the note taker may need admitting before recording can begin.
  • Botless recording needs enabling. If it isn't set up for your organization, Oliv Support helps your video conferencing admin turn it on.
  • Clips live in Enable. The clips route opens the Library tab of the Enable app.
  • Slack messages are on by default. They also go to your email. Turn them off in Profile Settings.

Common questions

Where is the Meetings list?

Under Activities › Meetings in the main navigation. The Activities menu also holds Calls.

How do I find a meeting from a specific day?

Search the Meetings list for the date. Oliv AI's help article uses a meeting held on June 12th as the example.

Can I search for something that was said?

Yes. Search for the exact objection, competitor mention or pricing concern, then clip the moment into a clip library.

Can I see my team's meetings?

Yes. Switch from My meetings to Team meetings to see meetings beyond your own calls.

What does each row show?

The meeting title, date and time, its duration, who it was with, the next step and the start of the AI summary.

Are phone calls in the Meetings list?

No. Captured phone calls have their own grid under Activities › Calls.

How do I open a meeting from Slack?

Press Open Meeting in the Slack message about that meeting. Oliv AI opens the meeting for you.

When does the list of tomorrow's meetings arrive?

At 4 PM local time the day before, once Oliv AI is connected to Slack.

When does the prep summary arrive?

Ten minutes before the meeting. It shows the meeting details and research on the account and contact.

Can I stop the Slack messages?

Yes. Turn them off on your Profile Settings page.

Do I need to set up these messages?

No. They start automatically once Slack is connected, and they also go to your email by default.

Why did a meeting not record?

If someone outside your company organized it, the note taker may have been waiting in the lobby. It needs admitting before recording can begin.
