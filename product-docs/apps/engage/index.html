What the Engage app is

Engage is where your reps prioritize, sequence, multi-thread, call and follow up, with the full account context attached. The product page puts its job plainly: it turns the context your team already creates into a ranked, executable workflow.

That context comes from the same record the rest of Oliv AI uses. Calls, emails, CRM data, Slack and meetings form one account context that Engage and every agent can read. So a rank in Engage can reflect what a buyer said on a call last week, not only what sits in a sequence tool.

Where Engage lives in the product

  • Apps › Engage opens the Engage home.
  • Plays and sprints each open on their own page inside the app.
  • Dialer sessions start from a new-session screen and run in an active-session view.

Engage the add-on

Engage is an add-on, not a plan. You add it to Converse, Sell or Grow for the team members who connect with prospects and customers. Each Engage seat includes one connected primary mailbox and LinkedIn automation. For current prices, see Oliv AI pricing.

Why the Engage app matters

What it's for

Engage is for outbound teams that need to work the right accounts each day, with a reason to reach out. It gives SDRs, BDRs and their managers one workspace for ranking, outreach and calls.

How it helps

  • A ranked list with reasons. Every account carries its rank and the reason for it.
  • A next move, not only a task. Each account comes with a recommended next step for the rep.
  • Context from the whole account. Ranks draw on CRM, calls and Slack, not only activity inside one tool.
  • A list that keeps up. The list re-ranks the moment a signal changes.

What happens without it

Signals sit scattered across calls, email, the CRM and meetings. The next step still comes down to rep judgment, and personalization starts with a rep researching and guessing. Activity gets logged, but nothing learns what moves deals forward.

Why buyers care

Sales development leaders care when a rep's book of named accounts grows past what one person can research. Oliv AI's sales development page puts it as 500 named accounts, of which a rep reaches maybe 50. Managers care when they can't tell whether reps are working the right accounts.

The Oliv AI approach to the Engage app

Picture an SDR manager who runs a call block every morning for a team of eight. Each rep carries a few hundred named accounts and two or three outbound plays at once. Before the block starts, the manager wants every rep dialing the accounts that matter this week, with a reason to call each one.

The usual answer is a sequence tool, a dialer and a spreadsheet. Reps spend the first part of the morning researching, and the list they work from may have been sorted days ago. A good part of the call block can go to preparation rather than conversation.

Buyers now leave signals in many places. They show up on calls, in email threads, in CRM updates and in Slack. A list that ranks accounts only from activity inside one tool will miss most of them, and it goes stale as soon as something changes.

Oliv AI ranks every account by ICP fit, engagement and call activity, and shows the reason for each rank. The list re-ranks the moment a signal changes, so the manager sees whether reps are on the right accounts. Oliv AI's Prospector agent writes the outreach, and the dialer puts a tailored call plan beside each contact.

The Engage add-on names its channels on the pricing page: email, LinkedIn, the dialer, and WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram. Those channels, with the ranked list, are the columns in How the Engage app compares, below.

What Engage does

Account prioritization

Engage ranks every account by what matters now.

  1. Ranked by ICP fit, engagement and call activity. The rank uses the whole account, CRM, calls and Slack included.
  2. The reason, shown. Every account shows why it sits where it does.
  3. A recommended next move. Reps get a next step that's ready to act on, not just an assignment.
  4. A manager view. It flags whether reps are working the right accounts.
  5. Live re-ranking. The list updates the moment a signal changes.

Email and LinkedIn outreach

Each Engage seat includes one connected primary mailbox and LinkedIn automation. You manage your sending setup from the same workspace as your sequences and follow-ups. When a team needs more volume, you add more warm email mailboxes. For outreach strategy, see the guide to outbound sales software.

Plays and sprints

Engage also holds plays and sprints. Each play and each sprint has its own page inside the app, so a team can open one and work it.

The dialer

The dialer is an add-on to Engage. It gives a rep one US phone number, with calling minutes to the US and Canada. A rep starts a new dialer session, then works through the contacts in the active session.

On the Engage product page, each contact in a session comes with a tailored call plan. It's split into parts such as an attention grabber and discovery questions. Oliv AI's sales development page describes reps starting a volume session at the parallel dialer, talking rather than waiting between dials.

More channels

You choose WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram one at a time, and each is added per person. Teams pay only for the channels they use. The Oliv AI pricing page lists each channel.

How Engage works with Oliv AI's agents

Prospector

Oliv AI's Prospector agent gives reps prioritized briefs, a point of view for each account and tailored outreach ready to send. The first email is always reviewed and sent by the rep. Follow-ups after no response are handled automatically by Prospector.

Researcher

Oliv AI's Researcher agent does the digging before every dial. Your BDR opens the contact list with briefs already in place. Before a first meeting, Researcher sends a three-bullet summary of the company, the person and an icebreaker. The prepare for a meeting guide covers research on new contacts.

Quick Qualifier

Oliv AI's Quick Qualifier agent watches for inbound leads from your website. It checks each one against territory and company-size criteria. It then routes qualified leads to the right rep, with a call script written before they pick up the phone.

Olivia

Oliv AI's Olivia agent coordinates tasks across your team. On the Engage page, Olivia builds the day's outreach queue by pulling in Prospector. The agents' work lands back in the same queue your reps already use.

CRM Manager and Forecaster

Engage reads the same account context as Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent and Oliv AI's Forecaster agent. Outreach and the deals it creates sit on one record. Keeping the CRM updated covers how activity reaches your CRM.

Who uses Engage, and how

SDRs and BDRs

An SDR logs in to a ranked list with the reason for each account. Research and outreach drafts are already done, so the morning goes to calls. More on Oliv AI for sales development teams.

Sales development managers

A manager sees whether reps are working the right accounts. Each rank shows its reason, so a coaching conversation starts from the account, not from activity counts. For the call side of coaching, see Oliv AI's Coach agent.

Account executives

An AE uses the same workspace to multi-thread and follow up on deals. For expansion, Prospector watches existing customer conversations and alerts the right rep when a signal surfaces. See how AEs run deals with Oliv AI.

Revenue operations

RevOps defines the ICP and the territory structure, and Prospector runs the build from there. The account data comes from your CRM, such as Salesforce or HubSpot.

Why Oliv AI is the stronger choice for sales engagement

  • A reason with every rank. Engage shows why each account sits where it does, using CRM, calls and Slack. Gong's help center describes how to sort by CRM values such as fit score to decide where to focus. Compare Oliv AI vs Gong.
  • LinkedIn automation in every seat. Each Engage seat includes LinkedIn automation next to the primary mailbox. Outreach's help center says reps must manually copy and paste the AI-generated message into LinkedIn. Read Gong vs Outreach for how Oliv AI fits that choice.

Limits and things to know

  • Engage is an add-on. It sits on top of the Converse, Sell or Grow plan and is priced separately.
  • Calling is its own add-on. The dialer comes with one US phone number and calling minutes to the US and Canada.
  • Channels are chosen one by one. WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram are each added per person.
  • One mailbox is included. Extra warm email mailboxes are added separately.
  • Reps send the first email. Prospector's first email is always reviewed and sent by the rep.
