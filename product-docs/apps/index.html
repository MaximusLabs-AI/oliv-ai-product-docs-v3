Converse: every customer conversation in one record

The Converse app records and transcribes your team's online meetings, phone calls and in-person conversations. It captures email and Slack the same way. A summary and next steps are ready as soon as a call ends, and every conversation lands in one searchable record.

You find it under Activities › Meetings and Activities › Calls in the main navigation. Converse joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls automatically, and the Zoom integration covers the Zoom setup. The Converse page on oliv.ai shows the capture channels side by side.

The Converse pages

Each page in this section covers one part of working with captured conversations.

  • Find a meeting. The Meetings list keeps every captured meeting, on My meetings and Team meetings tabs. Search it by date or keyword, or open a meeting straight from the Slack message sent after the call.
  • Read one meeting. Meeting details shows the recording, transcript and notes, with the AI summary and next steps. It also picks out objections, pricing concerns, competitor mentions and product requests.
  • Reuse the best moments. The clip library is built from your recorded calls. You describe the moments you want, and Oliv AI's Content Curator agent clips them from every new recording. In the app, clip libraries sit on Enable's Library tab.
  • Choose how meetings are recorded. Bot and botless recording explains how Oliv AI picks a mode from who organized the meeting. When your company organizes, recording is botless; otherwise a note taker named after your company joins.

The Converse overview ties these together, from capture to search.

Engage, Forecast and Enable

The other three apps open from Apps in the main navigation.

  • Engage is the outreach workspace. Its tabs are Inbox, Dialer, Plays, Flows, Signals and Experiments, and the Engage app page covers each one.
  • Forecast has two tabs. Submissions shows who on the team has submitted and each person's inputs per period. Boards rolls the team up by target attainment, pipeline coverage, commit and most likely.
  • Enable also has two tabs. People gives a manager the team's progress against target and a pacing card for each rep. Library lists every clip library, each set to Public or Private.

How the apps and agents share one record

Converse feeds the same context graph the rest of Oliv AI runs on. Your CRM, your forecast and every agent read from the same record, so one captured call can shape several pieces of work.

  • Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent drafts the follow-up email and proposes CRM updates from what was said.
  • Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent attaches the summary and transcript to the opportunity in Salesforce or HubSpot.
  • Oliv AI's Coach agent scores recorded calls against your scorecards to help reps improve.
  • Oliv AI's Content Curator agent builds the clip libraries that enablement teams share.

Meeting summaries and prep notes also reach your team in Slack. The agents overview explains how the rest of the agents fit around this record.

Which seat brings which app

Oliv AI sells seats by role, and the plans stack. Converse includes Amplify, Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell. So an SDR who only needs conversation capture takes Converse. An account executive who forecasts takes Sell, which adds the Forecast app. A customer success manager takes Grow, which adds the Retain app.

Plans and seat roles maps each person on your team to a seat. Prices are on Oliv AI's pricing page.
