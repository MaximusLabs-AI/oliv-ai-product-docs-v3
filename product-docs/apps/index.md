# Apps overview

The Oliv AI apps your team works in, starting with Converse for conversation capture, how its pages fit together, and how the apps share one record with Oliv AI's agents.

- Applies to: All Oliv AI plans; Converse on the Converse, Sell and Grow plans
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-21
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/

Oliv AI's apps are where your team reviews customer context: conversations, opportunities and accounts. Converse captures every customer conversation and makes it searchable. Other apps cover opportunity management and account management, and each person gets the apps their role needs.

## The apps at a glance {#apps-at-a-glance}

| App | What it covers | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Converse** | Capturing online meetings, phone calls, in-person meetings, email and Slack | Converse, Sell and Grow plans |
| **Forecast** | Opportunity management: opportunity grids, methodology tracking, forecast boards and submissions | Sell and Grow plans |
| **Retain** | Account management: customer health scorecards, churn risks and retention forecast boards | Grow plan |
| **Enable** | Evaluating customer conversations against coaching scorecards | Add-on |
| **Engage** | Email and LinkedIn outreach | Add-on |

Converse is the app these docs cover today. The [Forecast app page](https://www.oliv.ai/oliv-forecast) on oliv.ai shows its opportunity views, and [sales methodology tracking](https://www.oliv.ai/sales-methodology) covers the frameworks it tracks.

## Converse: every customer conversation in one record {#converse}

The [Converse app](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/) records and transcribes your team's online meetings, phone calls and in-person conversations. It captures email and Slack the same way. A summary and next steps are ready as soon as a call ends, and every conversation lands in one searchable record.

You find it under **Activities › Meetings** and **Activities › Calls** in the main navigation. Converse joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls automatically, and [the Zoom integration](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/zoom/) covers the Zoom setup. The [Converse page on oliv.ai](https://www.oliv.ai/converse) shows the capture channels side by side.

## The Converse pages {#converse-pages}

Each page in this section covers one part of working with captured conversations.

- **Find a meeting.** The [Meetings list](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meetings/) keeps every captured meeting. Search it by date or keyword, or open a meeting straight from the Slack message sent after the call.
- **Read one meeting.** [Meeting details](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meeting-details/) shows the recording, transcript and notes, with the AI summary and next steps. It also picks out objections, pricing concerns, competitor mentions and product requests.
- **Reuse the best moments.** The [clip library](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/clips/) is built from your recorded calls. You describe the moments you want, and Oliv AI's Content Curator agent clips them from every new recording.
- **Choose how meetings are recorded.** [Bot and botless recording](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/recording-modes/) explains how Oliv AI picks a mode from who organized the meeting. When your company organizes, recording is botless; otherwise a note taker named after your company joins.

The [Converse overview](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/#what-it-is) ties these together, from capture to search.

## How the apps and agents share one record {#apps-and-agents}

Converse feeds the same context graph the rest of Oliv AI runs on. Your CRM, your forecast and every agent read from the same record, so one captured call can shape several pieces of work.

- [Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/meeting-assistant/) drafts the follow-up email and proposes CRM updates from what was said.
- [Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/crm-manager/) attaches the summary and transcript to the opportunity in [Salesforce](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/salesforce/) or [HubSpot](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/hubspot/).
- [Oliv AI's Coach agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/coach/) scores recorded calls against your scorecards to help reps improve.
- [Oliv AI's Content Curator agent](https://www.oliv.ai/agents/content-curator) builds the clip libraries that enablement teams share.

Meeting summaries and prep notes also reach your team in [Slack](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/integrations/slack/). The [agents overview](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/) explains how the rest of the agents fit around this record.

## Which seat brings which app {#seats-and-apps}

Oliv AI sells seats by role, and the plans stack. Converse includes Amplify, Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell. So an SDR who only needs conversation capture takes Converse. An account executive who forecasts takes Sell, which adds the Forecast app. A customer success manager takes Grow, which adds the Retain app.

[Plans and seat roles](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/plans/plans-and-seat-roles/) maps each person on your team to a seat. Prices are on [Oliv AI's pricing page](https://www.oliv.ai/pricing).
