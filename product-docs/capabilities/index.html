Agents

Agents are the product. You talk to two of them, and they pass focused work to specialist agents.

The agents you talk to

CapabilityWhat it does
Oliv AI's Olivia agentYour Chief of AI Staff. You ask in plain language, and she answers or hands the work to a specialist agent and reports back
Oliv AI's Oliver agentYour Chief GTM Engineer. It studies how the team operates and keeps the playbook every other agent follows up to date

Specialist agents

Oliv AI's Deal Driver agentTracks deals, flags risks, sends a weekly pipeline update and loops in sales managers at key moments
Oliv AI's CRM Manager agentCreates and enriches accounts and contacts, updates fields and creates deals from your qualification criteria. You can map your custom CRM fields for it
Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agentPrepares you for meetings, takes notes, assigns action items and sends the follow-up email
Oliv AI's Forecaster agentRolls up call, upside, commit and best case, with AI notes on risks and changes

Oliv AI's Content Curator agentFinds the call moments that match your enablement priorities, clips them and sends them to the right people

Oliv AI's Researcher agentResearches accounts and buyers for ICP fit and context, and develops a point of view
Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agentTracks CSM portfolios across implementation, onboarding and success, and flags accounts that need attention
Oliv AI's Gold Digger agentFinds expansion signals across your accounts

The agent marketplace on oliv.ai groups agents by the roles they serve, and lets you pick a pre-built agent or create your own.

Common questions

Which capabilities come with every plan?

Oliv AI's Olivia and Oliver agents, reviewing conversations, opportunities and accounts, and building custom agents. Agent use is billed through credits on every plan.

Which plan adds forecasting?

Sell. It brings the Forecast app, with opportunity grids, methodology tracking, forecast boards and forecast submissions.

Which plan adds customer health and churn risk?

Grow. It brings the Retain app, with health scorecards, churn risks and retention forecast boards.

Can I build my own agent?

Yes. You can pick a pre-built agent from the marketplace or create your own, from instructions, tools, triggers and knowledge.

Can agents act without approval?

Only when you allow it. Each tool is set to Auto Run, Requires Approval or Agent Decides, and each conversation is set to Ask before acting or Act without asking.

How do I see what an agent did?

Open the agent's run history. Each run shows the people it touched, the emails it wrote and the changes it made.

Which meeting platforms does Oliv AI record?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, which it joins automatically, and Webex through the Webex App Hub.

Does Oliv AI replace my CRM?

No. Oliv AI works as a layer on top of your existing CRM. It brings the context together and keeps your CRM updated.
