Markdown

Rolling Oliv AI out across sales and customer success takes about a month, and most of it runs in parallel. Reps get their first brief before their next meeting, while your process handbook and the manager agents come online over the following weeks. This guide gives revenue operations a week-by-week plan: who runs the setup, which admins you need, what to switch on first and what to check at day 30.

Before you begin

  • Name an owner. One revenue operations lead runs the plan. A sales or leadership stakeholder checks the handbook for accuracy during onboarding.
  • Gather what you have. Bring your existing handbook, playbooks and methodology decks, even if they're messy or incomplete. Oliv AI's onboarding team works from them.
  • Find your admins. Some connections need an admin on the other side. The table in Step 2 lists who to book.
  • Decide your seat roles. Each person gets the plan that fits their work. Plans and seat roles helps you map your team before you invite anyone.
  • Don't clean the CRM first. Oliv AI reads what's already there, messy data included.
  • Know where Settings lives. Settings, at the bottom of the sidebar, opens Revenue Process, Qualification Framework, Coaching Scorecard, Targets, Measurements, Organisational Settings and Profile Settings. Steps 4 and 6 use most of them.

Step 3: Connect the stack in week one

Connect the CRM first. Oliver needs it for schema alignment, and every agent reads pipeline and deal history from it. Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho are supported.

Then connect calendars, your call recorder and Slack or Teams. Calendars tell Oliv AI which meetings are coming. The recorder is the main source of live context, whether that's Oliv AI's own notetaker or a tool you already use. Email is optional but adds context between meetings. Most teams are connected within a week, and the integrations overview shows how each connection fits.

Step 4: Build the process handbook

Oliv AI's onboarding team works with your revenue operations lead to translate existing playbooks, CRM configuration and methodology into Oliver's structured format. This typically takes one to two weeks. The handbook covers:

  1. Who you sell to. Your ICP and the signals that qualify an account.
  2. Who you sell to inside each account. Your persona library.
  3. How you qualify. The framework your team uses, such as MEDDPICC, BANT or SPICED, and the fields each stage requires.
  4. Who approves what. Pricing desk triggers and the deal size where manager sign-off starts.
  5. How proofs of concept run. What makes one eligible, what success looks like and how long it should last.
  6. How proposals and territories work. Review steps, approvals and who owns which pipeline.

Your stakeholder checks each section for accuracy. Once it's in place, every agent works inside those rules from day one. Oliver's proposed changes wait as Pending Review until your company approves them, and minor ones that apply on their own are still logged in the Playbook's History.

Set your stages and qualification framework

While the handbook is built, set the two definitions every deal is measured against.

  1. Open Settings › Revenue Process. Each sales motion has its own tab, such as Enterprise Sales, Mid-Market sales and Customer Success.
  2. Bring in your stages. Click Import Pipeline from CRM to pull the existing stages from the connected CRM.
  3. Check each stage. The table shows Type (Open, Closed/Won or Closed/Lost), Probability and the Forecast Category it rolls into, plus counts of Outcomes and Entry Criteria.
  1. Open Settings › Qualification Framework. The tabs are MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT and DRIVE, and the one in use carries an Active badge.
  2. Preview, then activate. Click a tab to see its criteria, scoring range and supporting fields, then click Activate to switch your organisation to it. Agents then score meetings and deals against that framework.

Step 5: Switch on first value for reps

Reps don't wait for the handbook to finish. Oliv AI's Olivia agent delivers deal context, risk and next steps 30 minutes before the next meeting on the calendar, with no waiting period. For meetings on an active deal, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent adds qualification status and a recommended agenda, as the meeting preparation guide shows.

At the same time, start Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent in Ask First mode. From the first meeting, call or email it proposes updates, and each one waits for review before anything reaches the CRM. Your reps learn what it gets right while nothing changes without them.

Step 6: Add managers and customer success

In weeks two to four, bring the manager agents online. Each has its own set-up time:

AgentWhat you setTypical time to live
Deal DriverConnect the CRM and team calendar, then set alert thresholdsMost managers are live within a week
ForecasterSet targets, measurements and your forecast cadence in SettingsMost teams are live within two weeks
CoachPick scorecards in Settings › Coaching Scorecard or bring your own criteriaFirst monthly review within two weeks
Portfolio ManagerConnect CRM, recordings, email, calendar and engagement dataDepends on your integrations

Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent works in the background, so reps don't need to change anything. Oliv AI's Forecaster agent needs no rep training; reps see a pre-drafted view where they'd normally submit by hand. Oliv AI's Coach agent needs about ten calls per rep to find a real pattern. For customer success, Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent builds a briefing of each CSM's book before every scheduled 1:1.

Set up the forecast

The Forecaster agent works from four settings. Set them in this order:

  1. Targets. In Settings › Targets, click Create Target, assign the users it applies to and track Overall Progress. Finished targets move to Completed Targets.
  2. Measurements. In Settings › Measurements, click Create Measurement. A Basic measurement sums or counts deals, and a Formula measurement combines others, as Pipeline Coverage does.
  3. Boards. In Organisational Settings › Monitoring › Boards, group your forecast boards into collections, each with its target and users.
  4. Cadence. In Organisational Settings › Agents › Forecaster, the Cadences table shows each cadence's board and the days reps and managers submit. Click Add to create one.

Add opens the New Cadence wizard in three steps: Overview, Boards & Measurements and People. On Overview, type a Title and Description, then pick a Period: Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly. With Monthly, you set a Rep Submission Day and a Manager Submission Day, each by week and day. Click Next to choose boards and measurements, then people.

Monitoring › Cadence then shows the submission plan week by week. Switch Period between Quarterly and Monthly, expand a period and tick which measurements are due in each week.

Choose Coach's scorecards

Settings › Coaching Scorecard is the library Coach applies, grouped into Deals, Customers, Meetings and Phone calls. Each scorecard has a description and its Evaluations (Last 30 days), so you can see which ones are already in use before the first monthly review.

Step 7: Review at day 30

  1. Check the CRM updates. Look at what reps accepted and rejected. Once accuracy thresholds are met, revenue operations confirms the switch to auto mode, as the CRM upkeep guide explains.
  2. Read the first coaching review. Coach's monthly review shows whether the scorecards match how you want calls run.
  3. Watch a pipeline review. Managers should open their 1:1s with Deal Driver's report, as in the pipeline review workflow.
  4. Tell Oliver what changed. Anything you learned in the month goes to Oliver as a process update. It waits as Pending Review until you approve it, then every connected agent picks it up.

What a deployment plan for Oliv AI is

A deployment plan is the order in which you connect tools, build rules and switch on agents, so each team gets value as early as possible. Oliv AI splits it into two tracks that run at once.

TrackWhat happensWho's involvedWhen
Value trackBriefs, follow-ups and proposed CRM updates start flowingReps, from their first meetingWeek one
Process trackThe handbook is built, models are tailored, manager agents switch onRevenue operations, Oliv AI's onboarding team, managersWeeks one to four

The tracks meet in the handbook. Oliv AI's Oliver agent keeps the rules, and Oliv AI's Olivia agent runs inside them for every rep. Behind both, Oliv AI tailors its signal models to your company. It researches what you sell, takes in your enablement materials and writes tailored prompts, so each model surfaces only the signals that matter to your motion. A tailored version is live within 1 day of the proof of value kickoff. For the wider architecture, read the RevOps guide to agentic AI.

Why a deployment plan matters

What it's for

A plan puts the right agent in front of the right team in the right week. Reps see value early, and managers get agents built on rules you've agreed.

How it helps

  • Reps feel it first. The first brief lands before the next meeting, so adoption starts on day one.
  • Admins are booked early. CRM, Zoom and Slack approvals don't stall the rollout in week three.
  • Rules come before scale. Managers get alerts and forecasts that follow one agreed process.
  • Trust is earned. CRM updates start in review, and auto mode waits until the numbers say so.

What happens without it

Without a plan, a rollout tends to switch everything on at once. Reps get alerts before the rules behind them are agreed. Managers see forecasts built on fields nobody mapped. The first impression is noise, and some teams never come back from that.

Why buyers care

This matters most to revenue operations leads, who own the rollout and answer for its results. It matters to sales and CS leaders too, who need their teams working in it within the month. It counts most when the contract starts close to a quarter or renewal season, because there's no slack for a slow start.

The Oliv AI approach to deployment

Say a revenue operations lead has just signed with Oliv AI for 25 account executives and a customer success team of eight. Leadership wants both teams using it by the end of the month. She has a methodology deck from last year, a CRM full of half-filled fields and one free afternoon a week.

The usual route is to clean the CRM, write the playbook down properly and then train everyone at once. Each of those steps can take weeks on its own. By the time they're done, the quarter has moved on and the team has lost interest.

Nothing about the agents needs a finished playbook before reps can start. A brief only needs a calendar, a recorder and a CRM. The rules can be built alongside, as long as someone owns them and reviews each change.

Oliv AI runs both tracks for her. Reps get their first brief before their next meeting, while Oliv AI's onboarding team turns her deck into Oliver's handbook in one to two weeks. If she picks We Run It, Oliv AI's operators maintain the playbooks on her behalf.

The proof of value adds one more piece: a tailored version of Oliv AI is live within 1 day of kickoff. How other platforms handle setup and tailoring is covered in How Oliv AI compares for rolling out revenue AI, below.

Who uses the deployment plan, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how teams roll Oliv AI out.

Revenue operations

A revenue operations lead owns the plan end to end. She books the admins, works with Oliv AI's onboarding team on the handbook and decides when CRM Manager moves to auto mode. Afterwards she keeps the rules current through Oliver.

Sales leaders

A VP of Sales signs off the qualification rules in the handbook. Two weeks later, first-line managers open their 1:1s with Deal Driver's report, and the forecast follows within the month.

Customer success leaders

A head of customer success brings the CSMs on in week one for briefs and follow-ups. Portfolio Manager's 1:1 briefings follow once recordings and CRM data are flowing.

Enablement

An enablement manager picks Coach's starting scorecards and reads the first monthly review. It's a quick way to see where new hires need help, as covered in cutting new-hire ramp time.

Limits and things to know

  • The calendar comes first. Oliv AI can't take notes in meetings until calendars are connected, so that step can't be skipped or delayed.
  • Some steps need an admin. Salesforce, Zoom recording without the host and, in some workspaces, Slack all need an admin on your side.
  • Handbook changes are reviewed or logged. Oliver's proposals wait for your company's approval. Minor changes can apply on their own, and every change appears in the Playbook's History.
  • Reps don't work with Oliver. Revenue operations and leaders do. Reps work with Olivia, who follows Oliver's rules.
  • Coaching needs call volume. Coach looks for about ten calls per rep before it reports a pattern.
  • Auto mode is a decision. CRM Manager starts in review. Revenue operations confirms the switch to auto mode once accuracy thresholds are met.

Common questions

How long does an Oliv AI rollout take?

Reps get value in the first week, from their first brief. The handbook takes one to two weeks, and the manager agents are typically live within one to two weeks of being connected.

When do reps see something useful?

Before their next meeting. Oliv AI's Olivia agent delivers deal context, risk and next steps 30 minutes before the next meeting on the calendar, with no waiting period.

How much of my team's time does setup take?

On the You Run It path, onboarding takes 2 to 4 sessions with your revenue operations team. On We Run It, there are no prompts to write and Oliv AI's operators maintain the playbooks.

Do we need to clean the CRM before we start?

No. Oliv AI reads what's already there, messy data included. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent fills missing or stale fields from call history as it goes.

Which admin access do we need?

A Salesforce admin for the package install, a Zoom admin for recording without the host, and a Slack admin if your workspace needs approval. Each user connects their own calendar by logging in.

Is there a proof of value?

Yes. A tailored version of Oliv AI is live within 1 day of the proof of value kickoff. Oliv AI researches your company and writes tailored prompts for each signal model.

Do reps need training?

Not for forecasting or deal alerts. Reps see a pre-drafted forecast view where they'd normally submit by hand, and Deal Driver works in the background.

Can we use our own sales methodology?

Yes. In Settings › Qualification Framework, preview MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT or DRIVE, then click Activate. Agents then score meetings and deals against the framework you chose.

When should CRM updates go automatic?

When the team trusts them. Updates start in review, and revenue operations confirms the switch to auto mode once accuracy thresholds are met.

Do the models keep up after launch?

Yes. Human feedback and quarterly re-research track new offerings, positioning and buyer personas as your company changes.
