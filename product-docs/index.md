## What Oliv AI is {#what-oliv-ai-is}

Oliv AI builds AI agents for revenue teams. It works as a layer on top of the CRM you already use, not as a replacement for it. The agents work alongside your team to prospect, close, retain and expand revenue.

You ask for work in plain language. [Oliv AI's Olivia agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/olivia/) runs the day-to-day work and hands tasks to specialist agents. [Oliv AI's Oliver agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/oliver/) keeps the rules: your stages, ownership and handoffs.

Everything starts from one sidebar: **Home**, **Agents**, **Apps**, **Reports**, **Records** and **Activities**, with **Playbook** and **Settings** at the bottom. **Home** has two tabs. **Ask** is where you talk to Olivia or Oliver, and **Agent Feed** is one stream of what your agents have done.

## Agents {#agents}

Agents are the product. Each one owns a part of the revenue workflow. [Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/deal-driver/) watches the pipeline and tells managers which deals need attention. [Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/crm-manager/) fills CRM fields from calls, emails and meetings, and you can [map your custom fields](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/crm-manager/field-mapping/) so updates land in the right place.

Each agent posts its work to the **Agent Feed**, which you can filter by team, role, agent and time.

[Oliv AI's Forecaster agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/forecaster/) drafts each rep's forecast, and [Oliv AI's Coach agent](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/coach/) scores calls and sets a goal for each rep.

## How an agent is built {#agent-builder}

**Agents** in the sidebar opens the marketplace, where you filter agents by team or search by the job they do.

![The Agents marketplace on the All tab, with filter pills for All, Our Agents, Sales, Revenue Operations and Customer Success, a search box, a grid of agent cards with View Agent or Clone Agent buttons, and Oliver's chat panel on the right.](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/images/screens/agents-marketplace/oliv-ai_031_agents-marketplace_all-tab_nine-agent-cards-view-agent-and-clone-agent-buttons-category-filters-search-oliver-chat-panel-open.webp)

The [agent builder](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/) shows what each agent is made of. Its [tools and run modes](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/tools-and-run-modes/) decide which actions run on their own and which wait for approval. [Triggers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/triggers/) start an agent when something happens, [knowledge files](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/knowledge/) give it your material, [instructions](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/instructions/) spell out its process, and [run history](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/agents/builder/run-history/) records what it did.

## Apps {#apps}

[Oliv AI's apps](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/) group the screens each role works in. In Converse, the [meetings list](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meetings/) and [meeting details](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/meeting-details/) hold every recorded call. In the sidebar, meetings and calls sit under **Activities**. [Clips](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/clips/) save the moments worth sharing, and [recording modes](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/oliv-ai-product-docs-v3/product-docs/apps/converse/recording-modes/) cover recording with or without a bot.
