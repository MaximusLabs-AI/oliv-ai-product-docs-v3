Integrations overview

The tools Oliv AI connects to, from your CRM and calendar to Zoom and Slack, what each connection is for, and which ones have their own setup guide.

Oliv AI connects to the tools your revenue team already uses and works as a layer on top of them. Your CRM stays the system of record. Your calendar tells Oliv AI which meetings to capture, your video tool carries the recording, and Slack brings summaries and alerts to where your team works. This page shows how the connections fit together and which ones have a full guide.

On each deal, the Momentum tab plots what these connections bring in on one timeline. Its Activity Map has a row each for meetings, emails, internal Slack, external Slack and agent actions.

Your CRM

Oliv AI pulls in your accounts, contacts and opportunities, then fills them back with what customers said. Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho work out of the box. Additional CRMs are available on request, so contact Oliv AI's sales team if yours isn't listed.

  • Salesforce. A Salesforce admin installs the package for all users. The Salesforce setup guide covers the install, review settings and cleaning up existing data.
  • HubSpot. You connect from the HubSpot integration in the Oliv AI web app, then share your HubSpot URL. The HubSpot setup guide also shows Oliv AI fields on deal records.

Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent does the writing. By default every update waits for review before it reaches the CRM. On a deal, the CRM Updates tab lists each change under AI Suggestions next to the current CRM value, with Accept and Reject. The deal header also has a Sync to Salesforce button that pushes the deal to the CRM. CRM field mapping decides which field each update lands in, and the CRM Manager agent page covers approvals and auto mode. For how two-way sync enriches your records without replacing them, read about the context graph and CRM sync.

Calendar and meeting recording

Calendar integration is required. Without it, Oliv AI can't take notes during your meetings. You connect a Gmail or Outlook calendar by logging in to the Oliv AI web app.

Once the calendar is connected, Oliv AI joins Microsoft Teams and Google Meet calls with no further setup. Zoom uses the Oliv AI Zoom app. When someone from your company organizes the meeting, Oliv AI can record botlessly through the meeting platform. When an external organizer owns it, a note taker joins under your company's name. Bot and botless recording explains the choice. The Zoom guide covers both Zoom apps, including CRM fields inside the meeting window.

Oliv AI joins external calls automatically. You can manage that from the Oliv AI web app or from your Google or Outlook calendar, using the Chrome extension or the Outlook add-in. Everything recorded lands in the Converse app, and you can find a past call in the meetings list. It sits under Activities › Meetings, with My meetings and Team meetings tabs.

Slack and other channels

Once Slack is connected, three meeting messages start on their own: a summary of tomorrow's meetings, a prep summary before each call and a meeting summary straight after it. They also go to your email by default. The Slack guide covers setup and how to turn them off.

Agents post to the same channels. Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent sends managers its alerts, briefs and summaries through Slack, email or the Oliv platform. Oliv AI's Olivia agent works through Slack, Teams or the Oliv AI product itself, as the Olivia agent page shows. For Microsoft Teams, Oliv AI sends an invitation to a shared channel.

Other sources of context

Meetings are one source among several. Oliv AI also captures email from Gmail and Outlook, phone calls, and in-person meetings recorded with Plaud, a wearable recorder. For account data beyond conversations, Oliv AI connects to Snowflake, Databricks, Redshift and BigQuery. That adds product usage, support tickets, billing history and onboarding completion to each account.

These sources don't have a setup guide in these docs. Oliv AI's integrations page lists the categories it works with and has a way to request an integration. Context capture shows every channel Oliv AI captures.

How agents use the connections

Every agent reads and writes through the tools above. Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent needs your calendar, a call recorder and your CRM before it sends its first brief, as the meeting preparation guide explains. The CRM upkeep guide shows CRM Manager writing back after each call.

Agents you build use the same connections. Each tool an agent holds is one action in a connected system, and its run mode decides whether the agent asks first. The Add Tool catalogue lists seven systems: HubSpot, Salesforce, Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, LinkedIn and Slack. An agent's Data tab shows the sources it reads, such as Salesforce Deals, Contacts, Accounts and Leads.

Agent tools and run modes covers those settings, and agent triggers can start an agent when a CRM deal changes. Add Trigger opens a picker whose Most popular group lists 11 systems, among them Microsoft Outlook, Google Mail, Google Calendar, Salesforce and HubSpot, with a longer All triggers list below.

Seat roles decide which apps each person uses, so check plans and seat roles before you connect a whole team. For a wider plan, the RevOps integration blueprint covers calls, email, Slack and the CRM together.
