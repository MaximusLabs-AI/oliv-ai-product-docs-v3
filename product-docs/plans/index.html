Plans overview

How Oliv AI's role-based plans, add-ons and agent actions fit together, which apps each seat role brings, and where to find current prices.

Status
Live
Applies to
For revenue operations, sales leaders and anyone planning an Oliv AI rollout

Oliv AI is billed in three parts: a seat role for each person, add-ons for the people who need them, and agent actions based on usage. Seats come in four role-based plans, Amplify, Converse, Sell and Grow, and each plan includes the one below it. This section explains how the parts fit together. Current prices are on Oliv AI's pricing page.

The three parts of a bill

PartWhat it coversHow it's counted
Seat roleThe apps a person works inOne plan per person, matched to their work
Add-onsCoaching, outreach and extra capture channelsPer person, only for those who need them
Agent actionsTasks your agents completeBy usage, separately from app subscriptions

The plans and seat roles page covers all three in detail. It also has a worked example that sorts a whole revenue org into seat roles.

Seat roles and the apps they bring

Plans are cumulative. Converse includes Amplify, Sell includes Converse, and Grow includes Sell. You can use all four in one company and give each person the plan that matches the apps they need.

  • Amplify gives every role the super agents, Oliv AI's Oliver agent and Olivia agent, on the Ask tab of Home. People can ask questions of customer context, review conversations, opportunities and accounts, and build custom agents. The agent builder pages, starting with agent instructions, show what a custom agent is made of.
  • Converse adds conversation capture: online meetings, email, in-person meetings and phone calls. It is the conversation intelligence plan, and you can buy it on its own. The Converse app docs cover what it captures, and bot and botless recording explains how meetings are recorded.
  • Sell adds the Forecast app, with sales methodologies and deal risks, for account executives, sales engineering and partnerships. The Forecast app page shows its views, and sales methodology tracking lists the frameworks.
  • Grow adds the Retain app, with customer health scorecards and churn risks, for customer success, account management, solution engineering and onboarding. More on Oliv AI for customer success and account management teams.

Add-ons

Add-ons are priced separately from the four plans and added per person. Enable evaluates customer conversations against coaching scorecards to find where a rep needs help. Engage runs email and LinkedIn outreach from Oliv AI, with a dialer, messaging channels and extra mailboxes available on top. In the app, Engage has Inbox, Dialer, Plays, Flows, Signals and Experiments tabs, and Enable has Library (clip libraries) and People tabs.

Capture add-ons extend Converse, Sell and Grow. Bot-based meeting capture sends a meeting assistant into the call, including externally hosted meetings when permitted. Slack, WhatsApp and LinkedIn capture are listed as add-ons too. Plaud hardware for in-person recording is purchased separately. The add-ons section lists what each one includes.

Agent actions

An agent action is a task an agent completes, such as researching an account or updating a CRM record. Agent actions are billed separately from app subscriptions, on every plan, and your total depends on agent usage. During a proof of concept, Oliv AI estimates agent spend at your expected volume and measures the value before rollout.

The pricing page gives example actions for several agents. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent creates records, updates fields and syncs next steps. Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent reviews pipeline, drafts follow-ups and flags risks. Oliv AI's Forecaster agent prepares the forecast and updates forecast categories. To see what an agent did on each run, open its run history. Each tool's run mode decides whether an action needs a person's approval first, as agent tools and run modes explains.

Choosing seats for your team

Assign seat roles by the apps a person needs, not by job title alone. Ask four questions for each person. Do they run customer conversations that should be captured? Do they own or forecast opportunities? Do they own renewals, health or expansion for live customers? Or do they only read context, ask questions and build agents?

Inside the product, the Agent Feed on Home can be filtered by Team (Sales, Sales Development, Customer Success or RevOps) and Role (Contributor or Manager). Each view shows the agents that team and role use. Those filters are views, not seat roles, but they show which agents each group works with.

For an account executive's day on the Sell plan's agents, read running deals as an account executive. A sales manager's week is in the pipeline review guide. Oliv AI works with your existing CRM, so you keep Salesforce or HubSpot as your system of record.
