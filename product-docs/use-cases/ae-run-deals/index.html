Where Oliv AI fits for account executives

What it's for

Oliv AI takes the admin around each deal off the account executive. The time between calls goes to selling, not to rebuilding context or typing notes into the CRM.

How it helps

  • Briefed before you join. A one-screen brief lands about 30 minutes before each deal meeting.
  • Follow-up written from the call. The email draft comes from the actual transcript, not a template.
  • Fields proposed, not typed. You accept or reject each suggested CRM update.
  • Stalls flagged early. You get a nudge when next steps go overdue or activity drops.

What happens without it

Each call can cost 15 to 20 minutes of digging for context beforehand and about 15 minutes of email writing afterward. CRM updates depend on what the rep remembers. Qualification gaps then surface at the forecast review, when it's too late to act on them.

Why they care

The account executive is the champion, because the time savings show up before and after every call. VPs of Sales and sales managers usually make the purchase. It matters most for reps with a full calendar of meetings on open deals.

The Oliv AI approach to running deals

Imagine an account executive with four customer calls on a Tuesday, booked almost back to back. Two are with deals in late-stage evaluation. One is a first call with a new stakeholder, and one is a pricing follow-up.

The usual routine is to skim old notes in the few minutes between calls. Each follow-up then gets written from memory at the end of the day, and CRM fields wait until Friday. By then some details have probably faded, so the record shows less than the calls did. The next meeting on each deal may start from a thinner picture as a result.

Everything the rep needs is already on record: the recordings, the CRM history and the email thread. The real gap is timing. Prep is most useful in the half hour before a call. The follow-up is easiest to write while the conversation is still fresh in everyone's mind.

Oliv AI moves that work to the right moment. Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends a brief before each meeting on an active deal and drafts the follow-up after it. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent turns what was said into field updates for the rep to approve.

The brief is built to fit on one screen and take about 60 seconds to read, as described under Before a meeting. How this setup lines up against other deal tools is covered in How Oliv AI compares for account executives, below.

Before a meeting

Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends a brief about 30 minutes before every meeting linked to an active opportunity. If the deal exists and the meeting is on your calendar, it runs automatically. The brief covers:

  • Deal summary. Stage, amount, close date, last activity and next steps.
  • Qualification status. Where the deal stands against your active qualification framework, with gaps and open questions highlighted. Settings › Qualification Framework offers MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT and DRIVE.
  • Stakeholders. Who is in the meeting, what roles they play and what they care about, based on prior conversations.
  • Recommended agenda. Tailored to the deal stage and the qualification gaps.

Shortly before the call, Meeting Assistant also posts a call prep note to your Agent Feed on Home. For a first meeting with a new contact, Oliv AI's Researcher agent builds the brief instead. Oliv AI's Olivia agent also surfaces the right context before every meeting, such as deal status, stakeholder intel, qualification gaps and what was agreed last time. Read more on getting the most from an AI meeting assistant.

After a call

Once the meeting recording is processed, Meeting Assistant prepares three things:

  • A follow-up email draft. It covers what was discussed, what was agreed and the next steps, with dates where possible. You review, adjust and send. See how a strong sales follow-up converts.
  • Proposed deal field updates. Stage, amount, close date, qualification fields and next steps, based on what was said.
  • Qualification gap flags. New information, or notable absences, that change the deal's qualification status.

Each meeting's record in Activities › Meetings holds the results in seven tabs: Overview, Action Items, Meeting insights, Recording, Coaching, Attendees and CRM Updates. Meeting insights files what was said under each criterion of your framework, with timestamps into the call. It also says plainly when a criterion wasn't discussed, which is where the gaps show.

Review the proposed CRM updates

After every activity, you get a Slack or preferred-channel message that says it found updates for a number of fields. The meeting's CRM Updates tab lists what changed after the call: completed updates, such as a refreshed account or a new contact, and pending ones with a button such as Update Deal.

On the deal itself, the CRM Updates tab splits AI Suggestions from AI Updates and keeps an Updates History. Each suggestion shows the proposed value above the Current Value. Choose Accept or Reject.

A full history of how each field was updated stays visible. CRM Manager applies some updates and holds others for your review, and its post in the Agent Feed says how many of each. Once accuracy thresholds are met, RevOps can confirm the switch to auto mode.

Keep the CRM current without typing

Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent keeps accounts, contacts, opportunities, activities and tasks current from your conversations.

  • New people and companies. When a meeting, call or email involves a company or person not yet in the CRM, it creates the record and enriches it.
  • The right deal. It maps each activity to the right account and opportunity, including multi-threaded deals. If it can't resolve one, it asks you.
  • Tasks from next steps. It creates tasks from the next steps in meetings, calls and emails, and assigns them to the right owner.
  • Buying committee roles. It tracks each contact's role on the opportunity, such as economic buyer, champion or blocker.
  • Meetings that weren't recorded. It can prompt you for a quick voice summary and use that as a data source.

A meeting's Action Items tab shows the Next meeting, Next action and Next follow-up, then the tasks from the call with an assignee and a due date. Each task links to the moment in the recording it came from.

It works with Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho, as a layer on top of the CRM you already use. More on how Oliv AI handles CRM automation.

When a deal goes quiet

Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent nudges you directly when a deal stalls, a next step goes overdue or activity drops. That catches the gap before your manager needs to step in. CRM Manager also sends end-of-day reminders for open CRM tasks or unverified deal updates.

On the deal's Action Plan tab, the Next follow-up card shows how many days a follow-up is overdue, beside the next meeting, the next action and your task list.

To work a stuck deal, tell Oliv AI's Olivia agent something like "This deal has been stuck for three weeks." She engages Oliv AI's Re-Activator agent and surfaces a re-engagement strategy, as covered in the things you can hand Olivia. Limits and things to know

  • Active opportunities only. Meeting Assistant works on meetings linked to an active opportunity. Researcher covers first-time contacts.
  • It needs a recording. Meeting Assistant relies on Oliv Notetaker recordings and transcripts and a Deal Insights subscription.
  • Not a CRM. CRM Manager works on top of Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho. It doesn't replace them.
  • Pending updates have a deadline. If a rep doesn't complete updates within a defined window, deals can be deferred or flagged as closed-lost. Each org configures this.
  • One framework at a time. Briefs and meeting insights follow the framework marked Active in Settings, so switching it changes what every deal is scored against.

Common questions

When does the pre-meeting brief arrive?

About 30 minutes before every meeting linked to an active opportunity in your CRM.

How long is the brief?

One screen, about 60 seconds to read. It covers the deal summary, qualification status, stakeholder context and a recommended agenda.

Which sales methodologies does it support?

Settings › Qualification Framework offers MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT and DRIVE. One is marked Active, and each shows its criteria and scoring before you switch with Activate.

Is the follow-up email a template?

No. Meeting Assistant reads the transcript and drafts from the actual conversation. You review, adjust and send.

Do CRM updates go in without me seeing them?

Some do. CRM Manager applies some updates, listed under AI Updates on the deal, and holds the rest under AI Suggestions for you to accept or reject. RevOps can switch to auto mode once accuracy thresholds are met.

What if our CRM data is incomplete?

CRM Manager fills in missing or stale data from call history as it goes, so you don't need a clean CRM to start.

Does it create contacts I haven't added?

Yes. When an activity involves a person or company not yet in the CRM, CRM Manager creates and enriches the record.

What happens if a meeting wasn't recorded?

CRM Manager can prompt you for a quick voice summary and use that as the data source.

Will I get reminders about my deals?

Yes. Deal Driver nudges you when deals stall, next steps go overdue or activity drops. CRM Manager sends end-of-day reminders for open CRM tasks.

How long does setup take?

Connect your calendar, your call recording tool or Oliv's native recorder, and your CRM. Most teams are connected within a week.
