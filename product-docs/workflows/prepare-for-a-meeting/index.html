Oliv AI sends you a brief before your sales meetings, so you walk in knowing where things stand and what to cover. For a meeting on an active deal, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends the deal summary, qualification status, stakeholders and a recommended agenda 30 minutes before the call. For a first meeting with someone new, Oliv AI's Olivia agent researches the company and the person. This guide covers setting it up, reading the brief and getting more when you need it.

Before you begin

  • Connect three things. The brief draws on your calendar, your call recording tool or Oliv AI's own recorder, and your CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho.
  • Have the deal in your CRM. Deal briefs cover meetings linked to an active opportunity. First meetings with new contacts get a research brief instead.
  • Know your methodology. Qualification status is shown against the framework your company runs.
  • Don't wait for a clean CRM. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent fills in missing or stale fields from call history as it goes, so the brief works from day one.

Step 1: Connect your calendar, recorder and CRM

Connect your calendar so Oliv AI knows which meetings are coming up. Then connect your call recorder and CRM: your existing recording tool or Oliv AI's own recorder, plus Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho. Past conversations and deal records are what the brief is built from. Most teams are connected within a week.

Step 2: Let the brief arrive before the call

There's nothing to request. Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent sends the brief automatically before every meeting linked to an active opportunity, 30 minutes ahead of the start time. Oliv AI's Olivia agent works through Slack, Teams or the Oliv AI product, so prep shows up where you already work.

Step 3: Read the brief in about a minute

The deal brief is designed to fit one screen and take about 60 seconds to read. It has four parts:

PartWhat it tells you
Deal summaryStage, amount, close date, last activity and next steps
Qualification statusWhere the deal stands against your methodology, with gaps and open questions highlighted
StakeholdersWho's in this meeting, the role each plays, and what they care about based on earlier conversations
Recommended agendaWhat to cover, tailored to the deal stage and the qualification gaps

Start with the gaps. They're the questions worth asking while there's still time to act on the answers, which is the point of sales meeting preparation.

Step 4: Ask Olivia to get you ready

You don't have to wait for the scheduled brief. Open Home, keep the Ask tab on Oliv AI's Olivia agent, and type the call you're preparing for, such as "Get me ready for my renewal call at 4pm today." Olivia answers in the chat, and a collapsed Reasoning Trace row shows the steps she took.

Her answer runs in a fixed order:

SectionWhat it tells you
Deal at a glanceFour cards: deal stage, ARR, days since the economic buyer was last touched, and deal health
Who's Joining?Each attendee with their role, such as champion or a new addition to the call
Risks to watchWhat could go wrong on this call, such as a silent champion or missing decision criteria
Talking pointsA numbered list of what to raise and in what order
Researcher briefA short profile of a new attendee from Oliv AI's Researcher agent: role, background and what they're likely to ask

A first meeting has no deal history to draw on, so the prep is research. Tell Olivia you're meeting someone for the first time and ask her to prepare you. Thirty minutes before the meeting, she sends three bullets and an icebreaker:

PartWhat it covers
The companyWhat it does, its size and industry, funding history and stage, recent news and market context
The personLinkedIn career history, current role and tenure, interests, and shared connections or common ground
The icebreakerOne specific talking point to open the conversation

For a VP or C-suite contact, the brief also covers company tenure and publicly known priorities. If you want more than the three bullets, the full detail view is in the Oliv AI platform.

Send a pre-meeting email

Oliv AI's Olivia agent ends her prep with a suggested next step, such as Draft the pre-meeting Email. Click it, and she writes the email two ways:

  1. Compare the drafts. One is marked Recommended, with a Why this version? box listing her reasons. The other is a softer alternative, tagged with when to use it.
  2. Choose how it goes out. Pick Send Draft 1 now, schedule it with Send Draft 1 at 3:30pm, Edit before sending, or Send Draft 2 instead. Nothing goes out until you choose.
  3. Read what happens next. Under After this goes out, I'll, Olivia lists her follow-through: log the touch on the deal through Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent, watch for a reply and ping you, and update your brief if none comes.

Step 5: Walk in with the agenda

  1. Open the recommended agenda and check it against what you planned to cover.
  2. Turn each highlighted qualification gap into a question for the call.
  3. Read the stakeholder notes, and decide who each point is aimed at.
  4. If a competitor is live in the deal, watch the clips pulled from past calls before you join.
  5. Join the call. After it, the follow-up draft and proposed CRM updates come back to you.

Why meeting preparation matters

What it's for

Meeting preparation puts what you need to know in front of you before the call starts. It covers where the deal stands, who's in the room and what to raise. The first minutes then go on the buyer, not on catching up.

How it helps

  • It arrives without asking. The brief lands 30 minutes before the meeting, with nothing to request.
  • It reads in a minute. One screen, about 60 seconds, scannable on the way into the room.
  • Gaps show up early. Open qualification questions appear while you can still ask them.
  • First calls get research too. New contacts come with company and person context and an opening line.

What happens without it

Without prep, reps rebuild each deal from notes and CRM records before the call, or walk in cold. Qualification gaps tend to surface at the forecast review, too late to fix. First calls open with questions the rep could have answered beforehand.

Why buyers care

This matters to sales leaders whose teams run dozens of calls a week. It matters just as much to SDR and BDR leads whose reps book first meetings back to back. It counts most when calendars fill up, because prep is usually the first thing to go.

The Oliv AI approach to meeting preparation

Take an SDR who has booked a first discovery call with a finance VP at a target account. The call is at 2pm, with three other calls before it. The goal is to qualify the account and hand a live opportunity to an account executive.

The usual prep is a quick tour of LinkedIn, the company's website and a news search, plus any notes in the CRM. Reps who do this well tend to spend 15 to 20 minutes per contact. On a full day that time often isn't there, so the research gets skimmed or skipped.

A first call is mostly won or lost in its opening minutes. The SDR doesn't need everything about the account, just the few facts that make the opening specific. That's a short job if someone else has already done the reading.

Oliv AI does the reading before the SDR has to. Oliv AI's Olivia agent sends three bullets on the company and the person, plus one icebreaker to open with. Funding, market position and recent news cover the account. Career history, role and interests cover the person.

The research brief lands 30 minutes before the meeting, and the full detail view stays in the platform for anyone who wants more. How other platforms prepare reps for a first meeting is covered in How Oliv AI compares for meeting preparation, below.

How meeting preparation works with other features

Follow-up after the call

Once the recording is processed, Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent drafts the follow-up email from the actual transcript, not a template. It also proposes CRM updates for stage, amount, close date, qualification fields and next steps. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent writes those updates, so the next brief starts from current data.

Clips from past calls

Oliv AI's Content Curator agent reviews every new recording and picks out clips that match a brief you set. When a competitor comes up in a live deal, Meeting Assistant pulls three recent clips from the competitive playlist before the next meeting, showing how other reps handled that comparison.

Manager one-on-ones

Managers get prep too. In Reports › My Reports, Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent delivers a Morning Brief every weekday. It covers the day's meetings, the ones marked important, and each rep's meetings with the deal behind them.

Each meeting card in the brief has Add me to this call, Leave a note and Review Deal, and a line comparing the meeting with your own calendar. Deal Driver also prepares a One-on-One Prep report for each rep, on that rep's own day, such as every Thursday or every Friday. Oliv AI's Coach agent follows minutes later with that rep's coaching goal.

On customer success teams, Oliv AI's Portfolio Manager agent builds a briefing of each CSM's book before every scheduled 1:1.

Qualification methodology

The qualification status in each brief follows the framework your team runs, whether that's MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT or DRIVE, set as Active in Settings › Qualification Framework. See how Oliv AI scores calls against your sales methodology.

Agent tools

The actions agents take in your calendar, mailbox and CRM are listed on each agent's Tools tab. Agent tools and run modes explains how to choose which of those actions wait for your approval.

Who uses meeting preparation, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the briefs are used.

Sales development

A sales development team books first meetings every day. Each rep reads the research brief between calls and opens with the icebreaker, so no first call starts with "tell me about your company." It's one way to keep AI-assisted prospecting personal.

Account executives

An account executive with a full pipeline reads the deal brief before each call. The qualification gaps become the questions for that meeting, and the stakeholder notes show who needs which point.

Account managers

An account manager walks into a renewal or expansion call with the full account history ready. Oliv AI's Olivia agent has it assembled before the call, including what was agreed last time.

Sales managers

A sales manager starts the day with the Morning Brief, then opens each rep's 1:1 with their pipeline, activity and flagged deals already on the page. The conversation starts at strategy instead of status updates.

Limits and things to know

  • Deal briefs need a deal. The deal brief covers meetings linked to an active opportunity in your CRM. Meetings with new contacts get the research brief instead.
  • Connections come first. No brief arrives until your calendar, recorder and CRM are connected. Most teams are connected within a week.
  • The research brief is short by design. It's three bullets and an icebreaker. The full detail view is in the platform.
  • Emails wait for your choice. A pre-meeting or follow-up email goes out only when you pick a send option or press send.

Common questions

When does the brief arrive?

Thirty minutes before the meeting. You don't need to request it; it's sent automatically for meetings linked to an active opportunity.

How long is the brief?

One screen, about 60 seconds to read. It covers the deal summary, qualification status, stakeholder context and a recommended agenda.

What do I get before a first meeting with someone new?

Three bullets on the company and the person, plus an icebreaker. Oliv AI's Olivia agent sends it 30 minutes before the meeting.

Which sales methodologies does the brief use?

MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT or DRIVE. Your team marks one as Active in Settings › Qualification Framework, and the brief maps the deal against it.

Does the brief include an agenda?

Yes. The recommended agenda is tailored to the deal stage and its qualification gaps, so it points you at what still needs answering.

Do I need a clean CRM before the brief works?

No. Oliv AI's CRM Manager agent fills in missing or stale fields from call history as it goes, so the brief can start straight away.

Where do briefs and alerts show up?

Oliv AI's Olivia agent works through Slack, Teams or the Oliv AI product. In the product, you ask her on the Home screen's Ask tab, and managers read Deal Driver's briefs in My Reports.

Is the follow-up email a template?

No. Oliv AI's Meeting Assistant agent writes the draft from the call transcript: what was discussed, what was agreed and what happens next. You review, adjust and send.

Can managers get prep for their 1:1s?

Yes. Oliv AI's Deal Driver agent prepares a One-on-One Prep report for each rep on that rep's set day, such as Thursday or Friday. Its Morning Brief covers the team's meetings every weekday.

Is there a minimum team size?

No. A single rep gets value from the first brief. Gap tracking becomes more useful as deal volume grows, because there's more for it to catch.
